# Session 8 Recap — Shadows of the Black Hand

**In-Game Start Date:** April 5th, 1241
**In-Game End Date:** April 6th, 1241
**Location:** [[Lektas]]

---

**[[Lucero De La Cruz]]** — a former field medic of the [[Order of the Radiant Cross]] turned drunken monk — joined the party after being briefed on the mission. [[William Cromwell]] welcomed him with a bear hug. The group then stormed [[Esaphir]]'s warehouse, where a decoy figure exploded before the real Esaphir dropped from the ceiling onto [[Kunrad Valancius]]. The party fought her non-lethally and wore her down, but **[[Baldwin Greystone]]** arrived with guards, knocked her unconscious, and took her into custody — refusing to let the party interrogate her.

A note found at the warehouse revealed Esaphir planned to visit **[[Darius Ralnor]]** — the soul-extraction mechanic the party had already killed. She doesn't know her benefactor is dead.

After a long rest, the party found **every prisoner gone** from the castle dungeon. A half-burned document claimed they were moved "off-site for safety reasons." Baldwin then produced a bishop's letter placing the party under his command and stationed them at the **Graybridge** with orders to hold the line. Six **mechanical soldiers** a newer, weapon-equipped model bearing the letter SM, formed a shield wall under **[[Captain Jirard]]**, who controls them via an orange-glowing gauntlet. [[Frigg Oden]] confronted Jirard about the soldiers being made from people; he seemed genuinely unaware.

Baldwin's voice boomed across the city:

> *"No quarter will be given to those who act in violence. Go back to your houses and you will be spared. Otherwise you will be put down like the dogs you choose to act as."*

Smoke rose across Lektas. A mob turned the corner toward the bridge.

---

*What will the party do when the mob arrives? Hold the line for Baldwin? Break ranks? Or find another way?*
