#Town

## Overview
Lektas is a massive city in the [[County of Phitalepia]] and the **primary port city** of the [[Kingdom of Pseizia]]. While the capital has moved to the landlocked metropolis of [[Athrelion]], Lektas remains the economic lifeblood of the realm, handling the vast majority of maritime trade. A sprawling coastal metropolis built on ancient foundations, the city commands a strategic position where a major river meets the coast. With over seventy-seven thousand souls within its fortified walls and the shantytown districts beyond, Lektas is a city of sharp contrasts—monumental architecture alongside squalor, and vast maritime wealth alongside grinding poverty.

The city's population is predominantly Muslim, with mosques and Islamic cultural institutions woven into the urban fabric. Lektas is governed by a Christian occupying authority installed after the crusades. The occupation has not been without its problems but was historically light-handed.

## Geography
Lektas occupies a commanding position where a substantial river meets the sea, nestled within the temperate deciduous forests that characterize the kingdom's coastal territories. The city's ancient walls embrace the historic core, including the elevated citadel that overlooks both the bustling harbor and the central plaza below. The river provides fresh water and an additional avenue for trade vessels, while the natural harbor offers protection for the kingdom's naval fleet. Beyond the walls, the shantytown districts have emerged organically along the riverbanks and coastal approaches.

The city's topography reflects its dual nature as both fortress and commercial hub. The citadel crowns the highest point, surrounded by the administrative quarter and the residences of the nobility. The plaza district occupies the central lowlands, serving as the nexus of commerce and civic life, while the port district stretches along the waterfront with its warehouses, shipyards, and maritime infrastructure.

### Districts
- **Silverside**: A poor district characterized by poverty and crime. Located near the city's outskirts.
- **Old Springs**: A commercial district with shops and warehouses. Notable for supply chain irregularities.
- **Graybridge**: A mixed district home to the Forum House, where locals gather for philosophical and political discussion.
- **The Port**: The crime-ridden dock area, handling trade from across the known world.
- **Rivers**: A district along the riverbanks.
- **Storm Church**: A district with significant Christian church presence.
- **Frostbloom Gate**: One of the major city gates.
- **The Outer City (Shantytown)**: Beyond the ancient walls, housing perhaps a quarter of the total population — immigrants, laborers, and those displaced by conquest.

## Economy
As the capital of Pseizia's maritime territories, Lektas the primary connection from Africa and Asia to Europe. The port handles everything from luxury goods and exotic spices to timber, grain, and military supplies. The city's merchant houses wield influence that rivals minor nobles, with trading companies maintaining factories and warehouses throughout the Mediterranean. Shipbuilding represents another cornerstone of the economy, with the naval yards producing both warships and merchant vessels.

The central plaza hosts markets that operate from dawn until dusk. Specialized quarters serve different crafts and trades: metalwork, textiles, pottery, and banking. The shantytown contributes through cheap labor for the docks, construction, and infrastructure maintenance. Despite the city's commercial activity, the wealth is unevenly distributed — the occupation has disrupted traditional economic networks, and districts like Silverside suffer visible poverty while the port and merchant quarters thrive.

## Culture
Lektas was built Millenia ago on Hellenistic foundations — its monumental architecture, theaters, gymnasia, and libraries reflect centuries of Greek-speaking civilization. However, the city's population has long since become predominantly Muslim, with mosques, Islamic schools, and cultural institutions deeply embedded in daily life. The occupied population maintains its own traditions, religious practices, and intellectual life, including philosophical gatherings at places like the Forum House in Graybridge where political and religious debate occurs in coded language.

The Christian occupying class maintains its own institutions — churches, military garrisons, and administrative offices, creating a layered city where two cultures coexist in uneasy tension. The shantytowns beyond the walls house the most culturally diverse population, where recent arrivals from conquered territories maintain their own traditions while navigating the complexities of occupation.

Public baths, markets, and taverns serve as meeting grounds across social classes, though the finest establishments remain exclusive to the wealthy and well-connected. The city's cultural life is rich but strained — festivals and public gatherings carry an undercurrent of political tension, and the line between philosophical discussion and sedition is deliberately blurred.

## Notable Features
- **The Citadel**: The ancient fortress crowning Lektas's highest point, housing the local garrison, the military command for the earldom, and emergency grain stores. Its massive limestone walls have never fallen to siege, and its towers command views of both city and sea.

- **The Grand Harbor**: Capable of accommodating over two hundred vessels, with dedicated military docks and commercial piers. Massive warehouses line the waterfront, and a great chain can be drawn across the harbor mouth to prevent enemy entry.

- **The Forum House** (Graybridge): An ostensibly philosophical gathering place where local intellectuals meet to discuss ideas. In practice, conversations frequently turn to coded discussions about overthrowing the Christian government and whether violence is justified.

- **Our Lady of Peace**: A Christian church in the occupied districts, serving the faith's community. The church has a cellar and private offices.

- **The Great Library of Lektas**: A royal institution housing the accumulated knowledge of the kingdom. Scholars from across the Mediterranean study here, and a staff of scribes continuously copies important texts.

- **The Naval Yards**: Extensive shipbuilding facilities along the river, producing warships and merchant vessels under direct royal supervision.

- **The Shantytown Districts (The Outer City)**: Beyond the ancient walls, housing a quarter of the population. Possesses its own vitality — vibrant markets, diverse traditions, and a reputation for both enterprise and lawlessness.

## Governance and Guilds
### 1241 AD
#### Governance
While **[[King Theodosios III]]** rules from the capital in [[Athrelion]], the day-to-day administration of Lektas falls to the local lord **[[Jeremy Finch]]**, who commands the garrison and oversees public order from the castle. The Christian occupation has historically been light-handed — the authorities showed little interest in exploiting the population, and the Muslim majority was largely left to its own affairs. However, recent months have seen a dramatic deterioration. Militant activity, unexplained assassinations on both sides, and the arrival of outside interests have destabilized the city. The garrison now patrols more aggressively, political institutions are monitored, and reports from the Muslim population to the authorities are routinely ignored.

The count of [[County of Phitalepia]], [[Earl Leonidas Chrysanthos]], maintains a palace in the city and serves as the king's representative in the Royal Synod, but is currently in council at Athrelion and is supposed to be for some time.

#### The Militant Crisis
A significant Muslim resistance movement operates within the city, led by **[[Nadir Al Salamai]]** (also known as Nadir El Salameh). The militants have been coordinating but have not made any major moves as of yet. The movement has significant popular sympathy, though its methods include violence against the occupation.

**The Party's Arrival (April 1241):** The party arrived to investigate Black Hand activities and received a church mission to eliminate [[Nadir Al Salamai]]. During their investigation they have contributed to the rising tensions in the city

#### Guilds and Other Organizations
The [[Merchant Consortium of Lektas]] represents the city's most powerful trading houses. The [[Guild of Master Shipwrights]] maintains standards for naval construction. The [[Brotherhood of Stevedores]] organizes dock workers. The [[Academy of Philosophers]] maintains scholarly traditions. The [[Temple Council]] coordinates religious observances and manages temple properties.
