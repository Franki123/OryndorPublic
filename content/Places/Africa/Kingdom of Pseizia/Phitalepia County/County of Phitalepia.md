## Overview

The Phitalepia Earldom is a major established province within the [[Kingdom of Pseizia]], serving as a vital coastal city. While it was once the premier province of the realm, that status has shifted to [[Chios County]], and the kingdom's capital has moved to [[Athrelion]]. However, Phitalepia remains an economic powerhouse, anchored by the massive port city of [[Lektas]]. The province is a center of maritime trade, naval power, and agricultural productivity, maintaining a critical role in the stability and prosperity of the kingdom.

## Geography

The earldom occupies the coastal territory along the kingdom's northern reaches, with [[Lektas]] commanding a significant natural harbor at the mouth of a major river. The province's coastline is rugged and defensible, while the inland areas consist of fertile plains and temperate forests. The region is densely populated, with the urban sprawl of Lektas and its surrounding shantytowns dominating the geographic landscape.

## Economy

Phitalepia's economy is centered on maritime commerce and the industries that support it. [[Lektas]] serves as the kingdom's primary port, handling the vast majority of international trade. The province is known for its shipyards, which produce both merchant vessels and warships for the royal navy. The inland areas contribute through logging and large-scale farming, providing the resources necessary for the urban population and export trade.

## Governance (1241 AD)

The county is ruled by **[[Count Leonidas Chrysanthos]]** (or a local lord acting as the Earl's representative if the Earl is at court in Athrelion). Governance is currently focused on maintaining order in the face of rising social unrest within [[Lektas]]. The Count commands the local garrisons and oversees the naval defenses of the southern coast, coordinating with the royal administration in [[Athrelion]] to ensure the security of the kingdom's northern borders.

## Settlements

- **[[Lektas]]** (Pop. ~77,117) - The kingdom's primary port city and the former capital of the realm. It remains a vital commercial and naval hub, though it is currently plagued by civil unrest and militant activity.
- **[[Emnos]]** (Pop. ~5,432) - A fortified lakeside burg that serves as a military installation and regional market town.
- **[[Aelea]]** (Pop. ~11,786) - A prosperous riverside settlement specializing in hunting and river trade.

## Culture

Phitalepia embodies the complex social dynamics of the Pseizian occupation. The Spanish and Italian-style Christian ruling class maintains authority from the citadels, while the predominantly Muslim population in the cities and shantytowns provides the labor and commercial energy of the province. This has led to a rich but tense cultural environment, where grand cathedrals and ancient mosques stand in close proximity. The people of Phitalepia are known for their maritime skill and their resilience in the face of political and social change.
