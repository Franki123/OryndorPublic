## Overview

The Kingdom of Pseizia is a Christian nation formed during the [[Second Crusade]]. Ruled from its capital, [[Athrelion]], the kingdom's policies are primarily concerned with maintaining order and control over newly acquired lands. The titles of the realm are relatively new, many awarded to lords for their service during the crusades.

## Geography

The Kingdom of Pseizia occupies a strategic position, anchored by its landlocked capital [[Athrelion]], a sizable city of approximately 78,435 inhabitants situated in the kingdom's interior. The kingdom's maritime power is projected through the primary port city of [[Lektas]], which commands a natural harbor and serves as the gateway for international trade. The geography is divided between established heartland provinces—[[Chios County]], [[County of Phitalepia]], and [[Heiaris County]]—and frontier territories.

The capital [[Athrelion]] is protected by a substantial citadel and encircled by massive defensive walls. Its central plaza serves as the administrative and political heart of the kingdom.

## Economy

Pseizia's economy is anchored by maritime trade flowing through the primary port of [[Lektas]]. While [[Athrelion]] serves as the political and administrative heart where taxes are collected and laws are decreed, the actual flow of international goods—spices, textiles, and metals—is centered in the southern shipyards and docks of Lektas. The established regions of [[Chios County]], [[County of Phitalepia]], and [[Heiaris County]] provide the agricultural and resource base that sustains these urban centers.

The frontier territories of [[Athmomissos Territory|Athmomissos]] and [[Laugardur Territory|Laugardur]] represent economic investments in future prosperity—requiring subsidies for military garrisons and colonial incentives while promising access to new resources, timber, and extended trade networks.

## Government

The monarchy governs through a hierarchical provincial system. [[Chios County]] represents the premier noble title, held by an Earl who serves as the king's principal vassal. This is followed by [[County of Phitalepia]] and [[Heiaris County]]. These lords owe fealty to the crown as part of their land agreement, providing military service, taxation, and local administration.

The frontier territories operate under direct royal governors appointed by the king, reflecting their status as actively colonized regions requiring centralized oversight. The royal administration in [[Athrelion]] manages diplomacy, treasury functions, and military coordination.

## Military

The Kingdom of Pseizia maintains a robust military force left over from the crusades. The **Athrelion Citadel** serves as the primary military headquarters and arsenal, housing the royal guard and coordinating naval operations. The kingdom's navy patrols vital sea lanes, protecting merchant vessels and enabling amphibious operations.

Provincial levies from the core counties provide heavy infantry and cavalry. The frontier territories maintain permanent garrison forces—a combination of professional soldiers and incentivized military colonists granted land in exchange for service.

## Provinces

The Kingdom of Pseizia comprises five administrative divisions:

*   **[[Chios County]]**: The premier noble province, held by an Earl who serves as the king's principal vassal.
*   **[[County of Phitalepia]]**: An established province providing trade access and naval support.
*   **[[Heiaris County]]**: An interior county centered around a large lake that contributes significantly to the kingdom's resource base.
*   **[[Athmomissos Territory]]**: A frontier territory under direct royal administration, actively being colonized and fortified.
*   **[[Laugardur Territory]]**: A northern frontier territory representing the limit of the kingdom's northern reach.

## Culture

The Pseizian ruling classes are a blend of various Christian European cultures, primarily leaning towards Spanish and Italian influences. The lower classes tend to be Muslims of Arabic descent. There also exists a debased Muslim upper class that has been largely excised from power, though they still hold significant influence among their brethren.

Architecture reflects this history; older structures are often Muslim in origin, while newer administrative buildings are built in a grand Spanish style. The current European nobility generally prides itself on the accumulation of wealth and efficient extraction of resources, often lacking the deep lineage of the original Arab nobility they displaced.

## History

### 1241 AD

The kingdom is ruled by [[King Theodosios III]], who has served as king since its inception. While he has been generally successful in maintaining order, the stability of the kingdom is frequently tested, especially in the frontier territories. Currently, trouble brews in the port city of [[Lektas]], where discontent among the local population is turning into open outrage, threatening the careful equilibrium of the realm.
