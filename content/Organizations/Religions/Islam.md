## Overview

Islam is a monotheistic faith founded by the prophet Muhammad in the 7th century. Muslims worship allah and follow the Quran and the Hadith. Islam is the dominant religion across much of the eastern and southern world and the primary civilizational rival to Christendom.

---

## The Prophet Muhammad

Muhammad was born in Mecca around 570 AD, orphaned young, and became a merchant. At 25 he married **Khadijah**, a wealthy widow who became his first convert and primary political backer. In 610 AD he reported receiving revelations from the angel Jibril in a cave on Mount Hira; these revelations, received over 23 years, became the Quran.

After Khadijah's death in 619 AD, Muhammad took multiple wives to build tribal alliances, neutralize defeated enemies, and secure political succession. These marriages were strategic — binding him to future Caliphs, absorbing rival clans, and consolidating conquered territory.

Muhammad was both a spiritual and military leader — he personally led raids and battles, making warfare an integral part of the prophetic example.

After his death in 632 AD, Islam expanded rapidly under the Rashidun Caliphs and later the Umayyad and Abbasid dynasties, stretching from Spain to Central Asia within a century.

## Core Beliefs

**The Five Pillars:** Shahada (declaration of faith), Salat (five daily prayers), Zakat (obligatory charity), Sawm (fasting during Ramadan), Hajj (pilgrimage to Mecca).

**On Non-Believers:** Islam divides non-Muslims into categories:
- **People of the Book** — Christians and Jews. Tolerated under **dhimmi** status: they may keep their faith but must pay the **jizya** tax, accept restrictions on worship and proselytizing, and submit to Muslim authority. Protected, but subordinate.
- **Polytheists and Pagans** — No protected status. The Quran commands Muslims to fight them until they convert. Islam or the sword.
- **Apostates** — Those who leave Islam. Prescribed death under the Hadith. Treated as treason and blasphemy.
- **Hypocrites (Munafiqun)** — Those who feign belief. Promised the lowest depths of hellfire.

**Jihad:** Armed struggle to defend, reclaim, or expand the domain of Islam (Dar al-Islam) against the domain of war (Dar al-Harb). Defensive jihad is obligatory on all Muslims when Muslim lands are attacked. Offensive jihad — extending Muslim rule — is a collective duty. Rules of engagement existed in theory but were rarely observed in practice.

## Structure

- **Caliph** — hypothetical supreme political and religious leader of the Muslim world
- **Sultans and Emirs** — regional rulers with varying independence
- **Ulama** — scholarly class; theologians, jurists, interpreters of Sharia
- **Imams** — prayer leaders and community teachers
- **Qadis** — judges administering Sharia law

---

## In the World

Muslim territories were the primary target of the [[First Crusade]] and [[Second Crusade]]. Cities like [[Lektas]] were taken and placed under Christian occupation. Muslims regard the crusades less as atrocities and more as obstacles to their own empire — the Christians are interlopers who will, in time, be expelled.

Muslim civilization produced advances in medicine, architecture, and philosophy, and preserved Greek and Roman scholarship. However, the Islamic conquests also destroyed much of the Hellenistic infrastructure they inherited — temples, statues, and shrines were systematically demolished to eliminate pagan worship.
