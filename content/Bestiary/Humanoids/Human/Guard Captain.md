> _"Kill the captain. The rest of them are just hammers waiting for someone to swing them."_ — Field note, recovered from a Black Hand operative

The Guard Captain is a human military officer trained to direct squads of [[Mechanical Guard|mechanical guards]]. He is not a construct. He bleeds, he tires, and he follows orders — because he believes in the chain of command, or because he has been told that what he does is necessary, or because he has stopped thinking about it altogether.

## Description

The captain wears half plate bearing the insignia and colors of the local garrison, a longsword on his hip, and a round steel shield on his left arm. At his belt hangs a brass command whistle and a short speaking trumpet used to project orders over the noise of crowds and combat. A thick leather bracelet around his arm holds a token — a forearm sized rectangle inscribed with the same arcane sigils found on the constructs' furnace housings.

The captains recruited for construct command duty are selected for composure, not conscience. They are men who can stand in a street and order a mechanical soldier to break a rioter's legs without flinching, then file a report and eat dinner. Some are career officers. Some are fanatics. Most are simply men who learned to stop asking questions. They do not need to understand the machines. They only need to know how to them commands.
