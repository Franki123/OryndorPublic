## Overview

The Order of the Radiant Cross is a zealous religious-military order integrated into the Christian Church. Founded to prosecute the crusades, the Order has since evolved into a standing force of investigative peacekeepers — armed agents dispatched to regions of unrest to maintain the peace they helped establish, largely through violence. Their authority comes directly from the Church hierarchy, giving them broad jurisdiction to intervene in local conflicts, root out heresy, and enforce ecclesiastical law.

---

## History

### Founding and the Crusades

The Order was founded as a militant arm of the Christian Church, purpose-built for holy war. During the crusades, its knights and soldiers served as the Church's primary striking force, conquering territory and suppressing resistance in the name of the faith. The recruitment sermons painted vivid pictures of defending the innocent — children burned in their homes, villages erased, a spreading darkness that would reach the homeland if unchecked.

The reality was far more brutal than the Church proper was led to believe. Many within the Radiant Cross turned a blind eye to the atrocities committed during the campaigns. Villages were "cleansed" on the basis of suspected insurgent sympathy. Field commanders ordered civilian populations into squares. Medics were told their oath was to the Cross, not to any secular code. Desertion during active crusade carried the penalty of execution.

The **[[Second Crusade]]** was a particularly infamous campaign. The Order aggressively recruited field medics and clergy to support operations in the eastern provinces. Events at settlements like [[4 - Places/Principality of Murionia/Naura County/Settlements/Phais|Phais]] left deep scars on participants — some stayed and justified the violence, others broke under the weight of what they witnessed.

### Post-Crusade Transition

With the crusades concluded, the Order has been repurposed by the Church into a peacekeeping and enforcement body. Its agents now serve as investigators, mediators, and — when diplomacy fails — enforcers. They are dispatched to areas of particular violence or unrest, carrying the full authority of the Church to restore order.

## Structure

- **Grand Commander:** The supreme leader of the Order, appointed by the Church hierarchy.
- **Chapter Commanders:** Senior knights who oversee regional operations and answer directly to the Grand Commander.
- **Knights:** Fully ordained warrior-priests who serve as the Order's primary agents — investigators, peacekeepers, and enforcers.
- **Chaplains:** Clergy embedded within the Order who provide spiritual guidance, conduct recruitment, and serve as the Church's eyes within the ranks.
- **Sergeants-at-Arms:** Experienced soldiers who lead field operations and command rank-and-file troops.
- **Field Medics:** Healers recruited or drafted to support campaigns; bound by oath to the Cross during active service.
- **Initiates:** New recruits undergoing training and indoctrination before taking full vows.

## The Schism of 1241

Starting in 1239,an ideological battle is being waged within the Order, threatening to tear it apart from within.

### The Veterans

The old guard — those who fought in the crusades and agree with the methods used. They see no problem enforcing their will on others through force and view themselves as holy enforcers of God's law. To them, the crusades were righteous, the violence was necessary, and the Order's current peacekeeping mandate is merely an extension of the same divine mission.

### The Reformers

Largely composed of clergy who were drafted into the crusades later in the campaigns and were appalled by the practices of the Radiant Cross soldiers. They seek to reforge the Order into something that can still be wielded in war, but on more "Godly terms" — with restraint, accountability, and genuine mercy. The Reformers argue that the Order's brutality has created more enemies than it has defeated.

## Activities

- **Peacekeeping:** Agents are dispatched to regions of violence and unrest to restore order, often serving as judge, jury, and enforcer.
- **Investigation:** The Order investigates threats to the Church — heresy, insurrection, and corruption among both clergy and laity.
- **Enforcement:** When diplomacy fails, the Order acts with force. Their authority from the Church gives them broad latitude.
- **Recruitment:** The Order actively recruits from both military and civilian backgrounds, including healers, scholars, and clergy.

---

## Reputation

- **Among the Faithful:** Respected and feared. The common faithful see the Radiant Cross as protectors of Christendom, though rumors of crusade-era brutality circulate in hushed tones.
- **Among the Clergy:** Divisive. Some view the Order as a necessary sword of the Church; others see it as an embarrassment that has strayed from God's teachings.
- **Among Veterans:** A source of pride for those who served — and a source of nightmares for those who broke. The existence of groups like the [[Brotherhood of the Broken Oath]] is a testament to the damage the crusades inflicted on the Order's own members.
