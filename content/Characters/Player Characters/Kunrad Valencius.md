## Reputation

Kunrad Valancius is a knight of the [[Order of the Radiant Cross]], known along the roads and border settlements as a grim, unyielding defender of the Christian faith. He carries a Warhammer and wears battered plate armor bearing the insignia of his Order, unmistakable to anyone who has seen it before. He is not subtle. He does not negotiate. He arrives in a community, asks what threatens it, and deals with that threat directly and with force. 

Those who share his faith describe him as a godsend: a protector who asks nothing in return, not even a bed for the night. Those who do not share his faith describe him differently: cold, judgmental, and dangerous in the way only a man of absolute conviction can be. 

He is known to have taken an **Oath of Poverty**, carrying no wealth and owning little beyond his sword, his armor, and his faith. He does not accept payment for his services. This makes him difficult to bribe and impossible to buy qualities that earn him trust from the devout and suspicion from everyone else.

## Associations

### The Order of the Radiant Cross

Kunrad is a sworn member of the [[Order of the Radiant Cross]], a militant holy order dedicated to the defense of Christendom. He trained with them from a young age and joined active crusade campaigns at age 8 as a squire. His standing within the Order is unclear to outsiders, though he is known to operate independently rather than as part of a garrison.

### Father Eamon

He has been seen consulting with **[[Father Eamon]]**, a senior priest known for his theological wisdom. Kunrad has often attributed his strong faith to the teachings of Eamon and his long hours spent together.

### Sir Alaric Trebid

Among soldiers, the name **Sir [[Alaric Trebid]]** often comes up alongside Kunrad's. Trebid is a veteran templar of the Order, reportedly a mentor figure. Veterans of the crusades speak of the two fighting side by side on multiple occasions.

## Known Conflicts

Kunrad has publicly clashed with several notable figures:

### **[[Malik ibn Rashid]]**

called the Desert Falcon — a Muslim commander encountered during the crusades. Soldiers who fought in those campaigns speak of the two as equal in both ability and zealotry, they are thankful they are on opposite sides. Malik was a great bane to the crusader force and Kunrad vowed he would kill him. He tried many times, in battle and in missions specifically to kill him. His last encounter ended in dozen of Islamic fighters dead, 25 of the 30 knights with Kunrad dead, the death of every resident in a village of 300 and Kunrad himself pinned to a stone wall by an iron spear that impaled him.

### **Baron [[Edric Von Holstead]]**

A Christian nobleman whom Kunrad has openly accused of defying Church authority. The nature of the dispute is unclear, though Kunrad has reportedly called him a heretic in public.

### **Lady [[Valeria of the Iron Circle]]**

A mercenary operator who is known to work for those of other faiths if paid, whom Kunrad considers a corrupting influence within Christian territories. He has warned communities against dealing with her. —

## Rumors

Those who have crossed paths with Kunrad tend to remember him, and the stories grow in the telling. Some say he has never lost a duel. Others claim he once walked three days through a desert without water, sustained by prayer alone. A few frontier priests insist he can sense sin the way a hound smells blood. What is consistent across accounts is simpler: the man does not smile, does not compromise, and does not stop. Whether that makes him a saint or a danger depends entirely on which side of his convictions you fall.
