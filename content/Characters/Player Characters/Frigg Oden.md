---

## Appearance

Frigg stands 5'7" with a heavy, sturdy build shaped by years of tannery labor and harsh winters. Her dark blue hair is usually in wild braids, framing sharp red eyes that seem to see too much. Her pale skin is marked with old scars and faint, unusual markings that some claim are ritualistic. She wears worn leathers, a weathered patchwork cloak, and carries a small etched dagger along with pouches of herbs and bone charms. She smells faintly of tannin, smoke, herbs, and something harder to place.

---

## Reputation

Frigg was widely regarded as a difficult and disruptive presence in Gotten. She openly rejected church authority, skipped sermons, and challenged both the pastor and village elders without hesitation. 

Rumors of witchcraft followed her closely though no formal charges were able to be brought. Even so she has been publicly denounced more than once. Despite this, some villagers would quietly seek her out for remedies, especially when traditional healers failed. Whether she is a skilled herbalist or something more unsettling depends on who you ask. She is stubborn, direct, and unafraid of conflict. Once she takes a position, she rarely backs down, regardless of the cost.

---

## Associates

**[[Klara Blum]]** — A young herbalist often seen in Frigg's company. The two are believed to share knowledge of plants and remedies. They are often seen in the forest together, and seem to feed off each other's energy.

**[[Mara Stein]]** — A hunter who occasionally meets with Frigg. Their relationship appears more mentor and apprentice than social, and Mara seems to have a calming effect on her.

---

## Known Conflicts

**[[Pastor Heinrich Kramm]]** — Frigg's long-standing opposition to the church has put her in direct conflict with Pastor Kramm. His sermons often condemn heretical behavior that many believe is aimed at her, though he rarely names her outright. Their tension is well known throughout Gotten.

---

## Rumors

Beyond the tannery, Frigg was often seen walking into the forests surrounding [[Gotten]] at odd hours, and just as often returning without explanation. Some say she simply forages, but most believe it is something darker. It is whispered that she keeps to old burial places, tending graves no one else will touch, and performs rites not meant for the living. Others claim she knows when death is near, appearing before or just after it takes hold, and that her remedies go beyond anything a [[Christianity|Christian]] herbalist would dare attempt. Whether that makes her a necessary healer or something far closer to death itself depends entirely on who you ask.
