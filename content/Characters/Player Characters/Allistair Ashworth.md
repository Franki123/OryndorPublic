## Known Identity

- **Full Name**: Allistair Ashworth
- **Occupation**: Bard, Traveling Performer
- **Affiliation**: [[Lanternbearers]] (active member)
- **Former Affiliation**: [[Ghostlight Troupe]] (trained; no longer traveling with them)
- **Origin**: Aristocratic; the Ashworth family name carries minor prestige in certain circles, though the family's social standing has waned

---

## Public Appearance

A tall, well-dressed young man with flowing dirty blonde curls and striking blue eyes that catch the light—and hold it a moment too long. He moves like a performer: every gesture slightly too deliberate, every entrance slightly too well-timed to be accidental. Carries himself with the ease of someone who has never met a room he couldn't own.

Those who speak with him often describe a faint, inexplicable unease—a momentary chill, a sense of being watched from just behind him. Most chalk it up to his dramatic presence. A few don't.

---

## Reputation

**Among common folk**: Beloved. Allistair is the kind of bard who plays the smallest village like it's a packed theatre. His audiences laugh, weep, and feel.

**Among innkeepers and merchants**: A reliable draw and a generous tipper. Spends freely, asks for little, leaves the room better than he found it. Known to stay an extra night if someone needs a song.

**Among the Lanternbearers**: A committed field member. Dependable in wartime regions, effective at lifting civilian morale. Keeps to himself about his past. 

**Among spirit mediums and the spiritually sensitive**: Unusual. The [[Ghostlight Troupe]] speaks of him with quiet respect—he has a rare gift, and he uses it responsibly.

## Known Skills & Traits

- Exceptional vocal range and stage presence
- Accomplished lutenist and composer
- Skilled acrobat (circus-trained)
- Fluent in the customs and manners of aristocratic society
- Conversant in funerary rites and spirit lore (Ghostlight training)
- Carries a lantern at all times — the symbolic mark of a [[Lanternbearers|Lanternbearer]]

## Rumors in Circulation

*"He can speak to the dead. I've seen him talking to empty air — and getting answers."* — Common in towns near Ghostlight Troupe territory

*"There's something off about him. Three people died at one of his old shows. He walked out without a scratch."* — Spread by [[Jasper Holloway]], source considered unreliable but persistent

*"The Ashworth boy was sold off by his own family. Bought his freedom through performance."* — Unknown

*"He's not as carefree as he looks. Ask him about where he started and watch the smile go thin."* — Observed by those who have traveled with him long enough

---

## Known Antagonists

- **[[Jasper Holloway]]**: A disgraced former [[Lanternbearers|Lanternbearer]] who was publicly exposed by Allistair. Now actively works to undermine his reputation—spreading rumors of "unnatural auras," bribing venue owners, and sabotaging bookings.
- **[[Maura "The Widow" Fell]]**: A spirit banisher who has taken an interest in Allistair's ghostly companions. Does not appear to be affiliated with Jasper, but her pursuit of him is consistent and methodical.

---

## What Is Not Known

The full truth of the circus fire that ended his former life remains buried. No formal investigation ever implicated him. His three closest companions — Faye, Isolde, and Toby — are listed in old records as casualties of a tragic accident.
