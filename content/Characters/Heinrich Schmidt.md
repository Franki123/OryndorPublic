A genius inventor and sole proprietor of [[Schmidt Mechanik]] a machinery company base in the [[Empire of Vogtsbach]].

Not much is known about the personal life of Mr. Schmidt. When speaking in public he talks mostly about his company, his machines or his experiments. When asked about his childhood, he responded with "I had the same kind of childhood that everyone else had." 

He is fast talking and extremely excitable, he views his creations as truly marvelous. a sentiment the world tends to agree with, since his machines are by far the most popular machines in all of Europe. They represent a clear paradigm shift from other machines and are remarkably efficient. Over the past several years, he has been working on mechanically operated prosthetics. 

This culminated in the production of the mechanical men, that were recently released into the world. When asked what led him to develop such a thing he responded:

"My work has always been to make life easier for those around me and I have been gifted with the genius to radically change how we view the menial tasks that we currently have to perform to stay alive. Why wouldn't I develop such a thing."
