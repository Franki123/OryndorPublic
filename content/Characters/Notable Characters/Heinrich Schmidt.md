**Occupation:** Machinist, industrialist, founder of [[Schmidt Mechanik]]
**Base of Operations:** [[Breinach]], [[Buchenbach Landgrave]], [[Empire of Vogtsbach]]
**Active Regions:** [[Buchenbach Landgrave]] and surrounding provinces

---

## Reputation

Heinrich Schmidt is a young mechanical engineer of extraordinary talent, widely regarded as the most gifted in the [[Empire of Vogtsbach]] — and possibly the entire [[Old World]]. He is the founder and sole owner of [[Schmidt Mechanik]], a rapidly growing industrial firm that produces agricultural equipment, mechanical devices, and — most notably — the articulated labor constructs known as [[Mechanical Man|Mechanical Men]].

He is not a nobleman, a guild elder, or a military figure. He is a machinist who rose on talent and capital alone. People who meet him tend to remember two things: his hands are always stained with ink, and he never stops talking about machines. He is socially awkward, visibly young, and utterly uninterested in the politics of the merchant guilds. He is interested in building things. Everything else is a distraction.

## Dealings

### Schmidt Mechanik

Schmidt Mechanik began as a small workshop in [[Breinach]] producing improved agricultural equipment — threshing machines, irrigation pumps, mechanical looms. The quality of the designs attracted rapid business. Within five years, Heinrich expanded to three factories across the [[Buchenbach Landgrave]] and built a reputation as a prodigy among the region's merchant guilds. His company now employs dozens of skilled laborers and smiths.

### The Mechanical Men

Schmidt Mechanik's most notable product is the [[Mechanical Man]] — an articulated labor construct capable of sustained autonomous work in mines, docks, and construction sites.

A lighter, weapon-equipped variant — the [[Mechanical Guard]] — is also known to exist, though details of its distribution are less public.

### Guild Relations

Heinrich Schmidt is not popular among the established machinist guilds and manufacturing houses of the [[Empire of Vogtsbach]]. His rapid rise, his refusal to join or submit to guild oversight, and his habit of rendering older designs obsolete overnight have made him a target of considerable professional hostility. Most guild-affiliated workshops refuse to supply him with raw materials or accept subcontracts from [[Schmidt Mechanik]]. Several have petitioned local authorities to impose licensing restrictions on his factories.

None of it has worked. Heinrich sources his own materials through independent suppliers, trains his own workers, and consistently delivers products that are cheaper, more reliable, and more efficient than anything the guilds produce. Orders that once went to guild shops now go to [[Breinach]]. The guilds talk about him like a disease — and yet they cannot seem to stop losing business to him.

### Business Partners

Heinrich is known to have a commercial relationship with a procurement firm previously associated with the late [[Darius Ralnor]] of [[Kalibethia]]. The current representative of this firm is not publicly identified, though dockworkers and logistics operators in the region are aware that furnace core shipments arrive on a regular schedule.

## Rumors

For someone so young, Heinrich has attracted a surprising amount of talk. Some say he is the illegitimate son of a provincial lord — though no one can name which one. Others say he has turned down commissions from the Imperial court itself because he refuses to answer to anyone. A few guild masters claim he memorizes entire blueprints after a single glance, and that he steals their designs with nothing more than a glance

Heinrich has never addressed any of these rumors publicly. He appears entirely uninterested in what people say about him, so long as they continue to buy his machines.
