> _"I didn't come on the crusade to be a hero. I came because they called, and you answer the call. But my back doesn't care about duty — it only cares about whether I'm standing or sitting."_ — Gregory McTubbs

**Occupation:** Soldier, castle guard
**Base of Operations:** [[Lektas|Castle Lektas]], [[Lektas]]
**Family:** Married, four children
**Active Regions:** [[Lektas]]

---

## Background

Gregory McTubbs is a career soldier who answered the call to join the crusades. A family man at heart, Gregory's ambitions on the battlefield were cut short by a chronic back injury that left him unfit for frontline combat. He was instead assigned to camp guard duty for the duration of the campaign — a role he performed without complaint, if without distinction.

Following the crusade, Gregory was reassigned to [[Lektas|Castle Lektas]], where he was able to transport his wife and four children to live with him. He serves as part of the castle's standing garrison under [[Jeremy Finch]].

---

## Reputation

Gregory is known as a dependable, if unremarkable, guard. He takes his duties seriously and is well-liked among the garrison for his even temperament. He is not a man of ambition or ideology — he is a man who showed up, did what was asked of him, and built a quiet life in a city far from home.

Among the garrison, Gregory is perhaps best known as a doting father and husband. He speaks of his children frequently, worries over them openly, and is rarely seen outside of duty hours without at least one of them in tow. His devotion to his family is the thing most people remember about him — more than his rank, his service record, or anything else.
