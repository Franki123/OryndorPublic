**Occupation:** Artificer, engineer, inventor
**Base of Operations:** [[Kalibethia]], [[Tanais County]], [[Kingdom of Phinonia]]
**Status:** Deceased — workshop fire, c. 1241

---

## Reputation

Darius "Steamhand" Ralnor was a well-known artificer based in [[Kalibethia]], where he operated a workshop producing steam-powered devices, mechanical tools, and experimental equipment. He earned his nickname from hands perpetually blackened with soot and oil, and from a habit of building things that hissed, clicked, and moved on their own.

Among the tradespeople and engineers of the [[Kingdom of Phinonia]], Ralnor had a reputation as a genuine prodigy — the kind of mind that comes along once in a generation. He was enthusiastic, approachable, and happy to talk about his work to anyone who would listen. Sailors, dockworkers, and merchants in Kalibethia all knew him by sight. He was a fixture of the town, always tinkering, always building something new.

He was also known to be generous with his talents. Local shipwrights and craftsmen sometimes brought him problems they could not solve, and Ralnor rarely turned them away. He repaired equipment, improved designs, and occasionally built devices for people who could not afford to pay, asking only that they let him test new ideas in the process.

## Dealings

### Ralnor Auto-Machine

Ralnor's workshop, known locally as **[[Ralnor Auto-Machine]]**, produced a range of practical inventions — improved irrigation pumps, mechanical looms, articulated tools for dockwork, and various steam-powered devices. The business was small but respected, and Ralnor handled most of the design work himself with a handful of apprentices and assistants.

In its later years, the workshop expanded into more ambitious projects. Ralnor began with articulated limbs — prosthetics for soldiers and laborers who had lost limbs — but eventually moved on to full-body constructs. By the time of his death, Ralnor had built several rudimentary **[[Mechanical Man|Mechanical Men]]**: walking, load-bearing constructs that could follow simple instructions. They were clumsy and limited, but they worked, and people in Kalibethia saw them. Dockworkers remember Ralnor testing them on the quays — iron-and-brass figures carrying crates or hauling rope under his direction.

The dock workers in [[Kalibethia]] took note that Ralnor was particularly proud of the **control circuits** in charge of the automatons. "Anyone can build a limb that bends" he would say "but making  a full body walk, balance, and carry without shaking itself apart is another matter entirely." Ralnor solved that problem, and he considered the solution his most valuable contribution to the field.

After Ralnor's death, the workshop and its remaining assets — including his circuit designs — were purchased by a company associated with **[[Tobias Fenwick]]**. The Mechanical Men that have appeared since, manufactured by [[Schmidt Mechanik]], are built on Ralnor's original control circuit designs, with improvements made to the Chassis by  [[Heinrich Schmidt]] himself.

### Boltar

Ralnor was publicly known as a close associate of **[[Boltar the Barbarian]]**, a former naval sailor turned pirate hunter. After Boltar was badly injured in a pirate attack, Ralnor took him in and nursed him back to health. He fitted Boltar with mechanical augmentations — replacement parts and enhancements that restored his physical capabilities and, by most accounts, improved on them.

The two were a familiar sight around Kalibethia — the wiry inventor and the scarred sailor, an odd pair but evidently close friends. Ralnor was openly proud of the work he had done on Boltar and spoke about it as proof that mechanical engineering could improve human life directly.
### The Benefactor

It was known — at least among Kalibethia's merchant community — that Ralnor's more ambitious projects were funded by an outside investor. Ralnor occasionally referenced a financial backer when explaining how he could afford materials and equipment that a workshop his size had no business purchasing. He never named this person. He referred to them only as his "benefactor" or "patron," and deflected questions about the arrangement with a smile and a change of subject.

The identity of this benefactor was never established publicly. After Ralnor's death and the sale of the workshop to a company linked to [[Tobias Fenwick]], some in Kalibethia assumed the two were connected — that Fenwick had been the patron all along. Others considered it a coincidence. Nobody pressed the matter.

## Death

Darius Ralnor died in a fire that consumed his workshop. The cause was officially recorded as accidental — an industrial fire, the kind of thing that happens in a building full of fuel, metal dust, and experimental machinery.

The official ruling satisfied the authorities. It did not satisfy Kalibethia.

Ralnor was well-liked. He was careful. He had worked with volatile materials his entire career and never had a serious incident. The people who knew him — dockworkers, shipwrights, merchants who had been in and out of that workshop for years — do not believe it was an accident. They will not say so to officials, but they will say so to each other, and to anyone who asks the right questions in the right tavern.

What they will also tell you is that the fire happened while a **group of travelling adventurers** was passing through town. Nobody can say exactly when this group arrived or when they left. Nobody saw them near the workshop. But Kalibethia is not a large place, and strangers are noticed — especially strangers who arrive shortly before the town's most famous inventor dies in a fire and then move on without lingering.

The townspeople have no proof. They have no names. What they have is a coincidence that sits badly, and a general suspicion that has hardened over time into something close to certainty: that whoever those people were, they had something to do with it.

A few other details feed the unease:
- The fire burned unusually hot. Parts of the workshop were reduced to slag rather than ash.
- Ralnor's body was recovered but badly burned and was thus difficult to identify.
- The workshop's hidden back rooms, which Ralnor kept locked and private, were completely destroyed. Whatever was in them was lost.
- No one in town has been able to identify the adventurers or determine where they came from. Descriptions vary — the memories are vague in a way that frustrates the people who hold them.

## Rumors

Ralnor attracted fewer rumors than some — he was too likeable and too visible for the sort of dark whispers that follow more secretive figures. But a few things were said after his death:

- That the prosthetics he built for Boltar seemed to respond to thought, not mechanics, in a way that no one could quite explain.
- That the locked rooms at the back of his workshop contained things he would not show anyone — and that he became noticeably tense when anyone asked about them.
- That his "benefactor" was not a merchant or a noble but someone far more dangerous, and that Ralnor knew it.

As with most rumors, the people repeating them do not claim to know the truth. They only claim that something did not add up — and that nobody with the authority to investigate seemed interested in looking.
