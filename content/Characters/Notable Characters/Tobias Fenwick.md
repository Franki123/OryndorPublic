**Occupation:** Merchant-broker, procurement specialist
**Base of Operations:** Unknown — no fixed address on record
**Active Regions:** All of [[Europe]] and [[Africa]]

---

## Reputation

Tobias Fenwick is a well-known figure in the overlapping worlds of legitimate trade and organized crime. He is a procurement specialist — the man you contact when you need something moved, something found, or something made to disappear from a ledger. His clients range from merchant guilds seeking discreet logistics to criminal operations requiring supply chains that do not leave a paper trail.

He is not secretive about his name or his face. He attends meetings in person, shakes hands, signs contracts, and conducts business with a calm professionalism that his associates describe as ice-cold. He is polite, precise, and never in a hurry. People who have dealt with him consistently report the same impression: competent, composed, and deeply unsettling in a way they struggle to articulate.

## Dealings

### Trade & Logistics

Fenwick is know to operate a procurement network that spans most of the known world. He brokers deals between suppliers and buyers, arranges transportation of goods, and manages supply chains of considerable complexity. Dockmasters, caravan masters, and warehouse operators across the coast know him as a reliable — if unnerving — business contact.
### Schmidt Mechanik

Fenwick has a business relationship with **[[Heinrich Schmidt]]** of **[[Schmidt Mechanik]]**, an industrial firm based in [[Breinach]], with the [[Empire of Vogtsbach]]. 
### Ralnor Auto-Machine

Fenwick was the business partner to the late **[[Darius Ralnor]]**, an artificer formerly based in [[Kalibethia]] After Ralnor's tragic death, caused by a workshop fire, Tobias (or rather one of his companies) bought the remainder of [[Ralnor Auto-Machine]].

## Rumors

For someone who makes no apparent effort to hide his dealings, Tobias has a great many rumors floating about him. Some rumors indicate that he can travel through walls or teleport great distances. Other rumors say that he can't forget anything that you tell him. Still other rumors claim that he is unkillable.

However, nobody has ever been able to confirm these rumors in any substantial way and so they remain the hearsay talked about in old taverns by old sailors.
