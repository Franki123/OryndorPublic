
> _"Blind? She read my books back to me, page and line, and told me which of my partners was skimming. She was right. She's always right. I'd lend her my last coin and I'd vote for her twice."_ — Spice merchant, [[Cytropodo]]

**Occupation:** Financier; head of Harrow House
**Base of Operations:** Harrow House, harbor district, [[Cytropodo]]
**Active Regions:** Cytropodo and the trade routes of the [[Kingdom of Tithonia]]

---

## Reputation

Lucia Harrow is one of the most respected names in Cytropodo's merchant quarter. She is the head of **Harrow House**, a trading and banking house that extends credit to half the waterfront, writes letters of exchange, and buys shares in cargoes bound across the kingdom. Harrow House pays on time, keeps its word, and has an uncanny record of avoiding ventures that later fail.

She is about twenty-five, slight and plain, and dresses in dark, well-cut wool without ornament. A fever took her sight at twenty-one, and her eyes are pale and clouded like candle wax. She doesn't cover them. In spite of her blindness, she moves through a crowded room without hesitation, and the waterfront has a saying for it: *Mistress Harrow never stumbles.* She reads through a secretary who is rarely more than a few steps from her side, and she has never been known to misremember a figure.

She is calm, exacting, and direct. Guildmasters bring her their disputes, and she has a reputation for telling powerful men exactly what is wrong with their books, down to the page. Widows and small traders trust her with their savings.

---

## Benefactions

- **The Harrow Dispensary.** A free clinic near the docks for eye complaints and wounds, open to laborers, sailors, and the poor. Mistress Harrow attends some mornings herself and blesses the patients with a touch to the brow. Given her own blindness, the cause is considered deeply personal.
- **The harbor lanterns.** Harrow House pays to keep the streets of the harbor district lit through the night. Theft and accidents on the waterfront have dropped since, and the dockworkers are grateful.
- **Parish gifts.** She is publicly pious and gives generously, but she holds no office in the Church. In a city where so much power runs through the Papal Court, that independence is unusual and widely noted.

---

## Connections

**[[Father Silas Vey]]** took her in as a starving child in his first parish. He fed her, educated her, and set her on the path to commerce. The two of them tell the story at charity dinners. It is one of the city's favorite tales of a good priest's kindness repaid.

---

## Ambitions

Harrow House has grown quickly through acquisitions. Several rival houses that ran into trouble were bought out by Harrow House, which kept their staff on, and that generosity is often remarked upon.

---

## Rumors

**The devil's bargain.** One ruined spice trader tells anyone who will buy him a drink that Lucia Harrow sold her eyes to the devil in exchange for the power to read any ledger in the city. He also claims the Consortium is run by lizards. Nobody takes him seriously, and most people in the merchant quarter find it a little sad that a man would blame a blind woman for his own bad books.

**The ghost of the lanterns.** Harbor drunks tell a story that St. Lucy haunts the new street lanterns and whispers to sailors who stagger home too late. The tale changes with every telling. Sometimes she gives warnings, sometimes she gives scolding, and once she apparently gave out a winning dice number. The priests have heard it in confession often enough to treat it as a joke.

**Candle dreams.** Some of the dispensary's patients report headaches afterward, and strange, warm dreams of candlelight. Physicians attribute it to the eye salves.
