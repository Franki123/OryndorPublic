# Rules for Intoxication

Any time a person takes a drink, roll the **Intoxication Die** for that drink (see [[#Drink Prices & Strength]] below) and add the result to the character's intoxication total. Compare this total to their Constitution score to determine the current level. Thresholds are calculated as percentages of the character's Constitution score (round down).

### Drunk Monks
Enemies attacking a drunken master with at least one level of intoxication have disadvantage. 

Additionally, drunken master's roll with disadvantage on intoxication rolls (roll two dice and take the lower result).

---

## Intoxication Levels

| Level | Title    | Threshold         |
| ----- | -------- | ----------------- |
| 1     | Buzzed   | 1 to ¼ Con        |
| 2     | Tipsy    | ¼ Con +1 to ½ Con |
| 3     | Drunk    | ½ Con +1 to ¾ Con |
| 4     | Wasted   | ¾ Con +1 to Con   |
| 5     | Blackout | Over Con score    |

*Example: A character with Con 14 hits Buzzed at 1–3, Tipsy at 4–7, Drunk at 8–10, Wasted at 11–14, and Blackout at 15+.*

### 1 – Buzzed

> *The warmth sets in. Everything feels a little easier.*

| Type      | Effect                                                    |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bonus     | Advantage on Charisma (Persuasion and Performance) checks |
| Detriment | Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws                   |

> *The edges are blurring. You're charming, reckless, and a little too loud.*

| Type      | Effect                             |
| --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Bonus     | Advantage on all Charisma checks   |

| Detriment | −1 AC                              |
| Detriment | Disadvantage on Dexterity checks   |
| Detriment | −2 to Passive Perception           |

### 3 – Drunk

> *You're swinging wild and talking too much. Your body isn't cooperating.*

| Type      | Effect                                                 |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Bonus     | Advantage on Strength checks                           |
| Bonus     | Cannot be Charmed                                      |
| Detriment | −2 AC                                                  |
| Detriment | Disadvantage on Dexterity and Intelligence checks      |
| Detriment | Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception and Insight) checks |
| Detriment | Movement speed reduced by 10 ft.                       |

---

### 4 – Wasted

> *You can barely stand. Coherent thought is a distant memory.*

| Type      | Effect                                                                                                                    |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Detriment | Gain the **Poisoned** condition                                                                                            |
| Detriment | Disadvantage on all ability checks and saving throws except Strength                                                      |
| Detriment | −3 AC                                                                                                                     |
| Detriment | Movement speed halved                                                                                                     |
| Detriment | Must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw at the start of each turn or spend the turn retching (lose your action) |

---

### 5 – Blackout

> *The lights are out. Someone else is steering now.*

The character is effectively incapacitated from rational decision-making. At the start of each of their turns, they must roll on the **Blackout Table** and take the resulting action instead of acting normally. They have no memory of events at this level.

Upon reaching Blackout, the character gains **1 level of exhaustion** that persists until their next long rest.

---

## Blackout Table

Roll a d12 at the start of the character's turn.

| d12 | Outcome                                                                                                                                                    |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Pass Out.** The character falls unconscious (stable) until they sober up or are roused.                                                                  |
| 2   | **Pick a Fight.** The character attacks the nearest creature (ally or enemy), using their full action.                                                     |
| 3   | **Confess Everything.** The character blurts out a secret, truth, or deeply personal information to whoever is closest.                                    |
| 4   | **Wanderer.** The character moves their full speed in a random direction (roll d8 for direction) and takes no other action.                                |
| 5   | **Crying Jag.** The character breaks into uncontrollable sobbing. They are incapacitated this turn.                                                        |
| 6   | **Give It Away.** The character attempts to gift an item from their inventory chosen by the player to the nearest person.                                  |
| 7   | **Bar Fight Champion.** The character shoves the nearest creature (Strength (Athletics) vs. their Strength or Dexterity save, DC 12).                      |
| 8   | **Undying Love.** The character declares passionate, embarrassing affection for a random party member. No action taken.                                    |
| 9   | **Vomit.** The character spends their entire turn vomiting. They are incapacitated this turn and must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution save or lose 1d4 HP. |
| 10  | **Serenade.** The character sings loudly and badly. Any Stealth check made by the party this turn automatically fails.                                     |
| 11  | **Arm Wrestler.** The character challenges the nearest creature to an arm-wrestling contest and grabs them.                                                |
| 12  | **Second Wind?** The character seems to rally briefly — they act normally this turn, but immediately pass out at the end of it.                            |

## Drink Prices & Strength

The intoxication die varies by the strength of the drink. Stronger drinks cost more and escalate intoxication faster.

| Strength    | Intox. Die | Example Drinks                               | Approx. ABV | Typical Cost |
| ----------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------ |
| Weak        | 1(always)  | Small beer, watered wine, perry, switchel    | 1–3%        | 2 cp         |
| Standard    | 1d4        | Ale, mead, table wine, cider                 | 4–8%        | 4–5 cp       |
| Strong      | 1d6        | Stout, porter, bock, mulled wine             | 7–12%       | 8 cp – 3 sp  |
| Very Strong | 1d8        | Whiskey, brandy, rum, gin                    | 35–45%      | 1 sp – 5 sp  |
| Exotic      | 1d10       | Absinthe, aquavit, grappa, aged cognac       | 50–70%      | 1–3 gp       |
| Legendary   | 1d12       | Overproof grain spirit, high-proof moonshine | 80–95%      | 1–5 gp       |

> **Note:** The DM may adjust prices based on region, availability, and the establishment. A dockside tavern and a noble's banquet serve very different pours.

---

## Sobering Up

Characters reduce their intoxication total by **1d4 per hour** of rest, or by **1** for every full meal consumed. A *lesser restoration* spell removes one full intoxication level. *Sobering herbs* (if available) reduce intoxication by 1d6 immediately.
