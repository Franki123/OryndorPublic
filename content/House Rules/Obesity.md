# Rules for Obesity

These rules apply to characters who **begin play as obese** or who are otherwise established in the narrative as carrying significant excess weight. Obesity is a hindrance -- it slows you down, wears you out, and makes combat harder.

A character's obesity level is chosen at character creation or assigned by the DM when appropriate. Each level applies independently.

---

## Obesity Levels

| Level | Title          | Description                                        |
| ----- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Heavyset       | Noticeably large, but still mostly functional      |
| 2     | Obese          | Movement and agility are noticeably impaired       |
| 3     | Morbidly Obese | The body is a constant burden                      |

---

### 1 - Heavyset

> *Your belt is tight and the stairs are getting longer.*

| Effect                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------- |
| -5 ft. movement speed                                    |
| Disadvantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks            |
| Climbing and swimming cost an extra 1 ft. per ft. moved  |

---

### 2 - Obese

> *Swinging a sword is easy. Getting out of the way of one is the problem.*

| Effect                                  |
| --------------------------------------- |
| -10 ft. movement speed                  |
| Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws |
| -1 AC (armor doesn't sit right)         |
| Cannot use the Dodge action             |

---

### 3 - Morbidly Obese

> *Every fight is two fights -- one against the enemy, one against your own body.*

| Effect                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------- |
| -15 ft. movement speed                                   |
| Disadvantage on Dexterity and Constitution saving throws |
| -2 AC                                                    |
| Cannot Dash or Dodge                                     |
| Squeezing through spaces costs an extra 1 ft. per ft.    |

---

## Combat Mechanics

### Winded

Obesity makes prolonged combat exhausting. An obese character tracks **Winded** rounds during combat.

- At the **end of each round** in which the character used their **Action to attack, cast a spell with a somatic component, or move more than half their speed**, they gain **1 Winded counter**.
- At the **end of each round** in which the character DID NOT use their **Action to attack, cast a spell with a somatic component, or move more than half their speed**, they lose **1 Winded counter**.
- After about 1 minute out of combat any Winded counters the player has are reset.

| Winded Counters | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3               | The character is **Winded**. They have disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of their next turn.                                                                                                                                           |
| 5               | The character is **Gasping**. They must use their bonus action each turn to catch their breath or suffer disadvantage on all attacks and ability checks.                                                                                        |
| 7+              | The character is **Spent**. They must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw at the start of their turn or lose their action for the turn (they can still move and use a bonus action). On a success, they act normally but remain Spent. |

### Crushing Fall

When an obese character (level 2+) is knocked **prone** on top of or adjacent to a creature of equal or smaller size, that creature must succeed on a **Dexterity saving throw (DC 10)** or take **1d4 bludgeoning damage** (1d6 at Morbidly Obese). This is involuntary and happens any time the character falls prone, including against allies.

## Armor & Equipment

| Obesity Level  | Equipment Effect                                                            |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Heavyset       | Standard armor fits with minor adjustments (no extra cost)                  |
| Obese          | Armor must be custom-fitted: costs **1.5x** the base price                 |
| Morbidly Obese | Armor must be custom-fitted: costs **2x** the base price and weighs **1.5x** |

Mounts must be **one size larger** than normal for an obese character at level 2+, or the mount's speed is halved and it cannot Dash.

---

## Travel & Exploration

| Obesity Level  | Effect                                                                                                              |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Heavyset       | No additional travel penalties beyond reduced speed                                                                 |
| Obese          | Must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution save after 4 hours of travel or gain 1 level of exhaustion                     |
| Morbidly Obese | Must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution save after 2 hours of travel or gain 1 level of exhaustion; cannot force march |

---

## Stress Eating (Bonus Action)

An obese character can use their **bonus action** to consume food (a ration, snack, or any edible item in their inventory). Eating in this way provides temporary relief from the strain of their weight, granting the following benefits until the **end of their next turn**:

| Obesity Level  | Effect of Eating                                                                        |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Heavyset       | Ignore the movement speed penalty                                                       |
| Obese          | Ignore the movement speed penalty and the AC penalty                                    |
| Morbidly Obese | Ignore the movement speed penalty and the AC penalty; may use the Dash action this turn |

This also removes **1 Winded counter** (if any).

### Limitations

- The character must have food on their person to use this ability. Each use consumes **1/4 of a day's ration** or one snack-sized item.
- This can be used **a number of times per long rest equal to their Constitution modifier** (minimum 1).
- This may impede the character's weight loss journey.
