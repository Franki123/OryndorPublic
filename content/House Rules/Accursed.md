### Condition: Accursed
> *Cursed and cut off: from God, from the Church, and from whatever host it called its own.*

Only a creature with **Intelligence 3 or higher** can be Accursed. It isn't a charm effect, so immunity to being charmed doesn't protect against it. While Accursed, a creature is:
- **Shunned.** It gets no benefit from any spell, aura, feature, or trait of its allies, and it can't send or receive telepathic communication.
- **Forsaken.** It can't take reactions.
- **Wrathful.** On its turn, a creature with less than 10 wis must move toward the nearest creature that isn't Accursed and attack it if it can. It doesn't retreat, use tactics, or choose its target.

**Duration:** 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the creature makes a **Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC** and ends the condition on a success. *Remove Curse* or *Greater Restoration* also ends it.
