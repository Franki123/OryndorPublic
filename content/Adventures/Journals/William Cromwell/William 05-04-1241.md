[[William Cromwell]]


I watched the militants approach, but I could not stomach the thought of fighting besides those unholy abominations that they call Men of Iron. Christ would surly not condone allying with such despicable things constructed from the flayed souls of his faithful. I walked away in disgust and headed to confront this Lord [[Baldwin Greystone|Baldwin]]. I may be walking to my death, but a Glorious death is preferable to living with the shame of allying with evil. As we approached the castle, [[Gregory McTubbs]] attempted to dissuade me, but I would not be deterred. He was flanked by 2 of those abominations. I attempted to explain the horrors of what they are, but he would not listen. Nonetheless, he stepped aside and told me where Baldwin was. I entered the castle, and confronted Baldwin. He tried to deflect my questions on useless pedanticism, but I would not be deterred. He gave me an ultimatum to either work with those disgusting constructs, or leave.

Before I could respond [[Frigg Oden|Frigg]] and [[Lucero De La Cruz|Lucero]] attacked. After a brief scuffle, [[Allistair Ashworth|Allistair]] arrived and managed to calm everyone down for the moment. As soon as I got fed up with his refusal to stop using the Men of Iron and started to leave, Frigg and Allistair seemed to be shocked by something, and Frigg started babbling about a demon or something.

Refusing to trust the consistently deceitful pagan, I decided to leave the city and focus on our primary mission. I still don't trust Baldwin, but we have greater priorities at the moment.
