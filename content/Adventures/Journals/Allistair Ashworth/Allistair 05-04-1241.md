## The Riots

It is only our third day in this forsaken city and I already want nothing more than to leave this place. It seems as though madness runs deep here, and it has affected both the party and this city's inhabitants. [[Baldwin Greystone|Baldwin]] sent us out to go and deal with the rioters. When we got there I couldn't help but feel sorry for them. They clearly just want to have their homes back without threat of persecution. Our party split shortly after we arrived and noticed that there were those fiendish constructs on our side. [[William Cromwell|William]], [[Frigg Oden|Frigg]], and some drunken monk whom only recently joined immediately left me and [[Kunrad Valencius|Kunrad]] to return to the castle to let [[Baldwin Greystone|Baldwin]] know their opinion about fighting along these monstrosities. I decided it would be better to stay. After all, [[Kunrad Valencius|Kunrad]] isn't exactly one to let up and I didn't trust that the captain and his constructs would spare any civilians either. [[Kunrad Valencius|Kunrad]] tried to give a speech to "calm" the rioters, but I'm not sure if he knows that his religious lectures about how he will put an end to their heathen ways aren't exactly the most easing. I tried to use the power of music to calm the crowd but it seemed as though Kunrad's words had already done too much damage and they were set on attacking. I felt their plea, they just wanted their homes back. To live their lives without the threat of persecution. And I wondered if I was on the right side here. After all, the side I was fighting with was using machines powered by the souls of the innocent.

## The Commander

[[Faye]] and I had a brief talk. At the end of which, I had made up my mind that I wasn't going to let the constructs kill all of these people. I had noticed that they were controlled by a glowing bracelet that the commander wore. I figured that if I took him out the constructs would power down and stop their assault on the rioters. While his body may have been fairly strong, the same could not be said about his mind.

All it took was a couple of whispers to break his resolve and cause him to jump into the river below. It seemed as though that stopped the constructs. Additionally, at that point the rioters decided to disperse from the bridge. I figured that I had done enough to protect them from harm and left to meet the rest of my companions at the castle.

## Back at the Castle

However, shortly after I arrived at the castle, a battered [[Kunrad Valencius|Kunrad]] had the nerve to run up on me and punch me. Not only that, he thought he had the right to then proceed to scream a string of profanities so extreme that I could not dare to repeat. So, I decided that I would give him a piece of my mind back, especially since he was yelling on and on about me abandoning him even when it was clear that the battle was over when I left. Luckily, he yelled his way to exhaustion. Saving both me and the rest of the castle from listening to his self-righteousness for a little while. 

After entering the castle I quickly discovered that the rest of my companions had entered into a fight against [[Baldwin]]. Which baffled me considering the fact that, while he was the one using the constructs, that does not mean that he knows all of the factors involved or that he himself is involved in their creation. It took a little bit of convincing to calm them down. Especially the ever violent [[Frigg Oden|Frigg]], who was adamant that she wanted to fight him and kill him for what appears to be no apparent reason. Since day one in this city she has been causing problems. Whether it be burning down buildings or just plain theft, that pagan does not do very much to lessen the stereotypes on her kind. 

Once things cooled down, it was clear that [[Baldwin]] no longer desired our service and just wanted us to leave. Which I much desired considering how terrible this city is.

## The Vision

However, that was when some horrible vision fell upon me. I am not sure if what I saw was my brain playing tricks on me or not, but it was more horrifying than any of my nightly terrors could imagine.

I do not know what it is I saw, but [[Frigg Oden|Frigg]] described it as some god of death. After learning this I was all the more eager to leave and decided to make my way back to the inn to prepare myself to get out of this city as fast as I could. However, the streets were not safe and we had to wait until the rioting was over before we could depart. I do not wish to know what the death count was afterwards. I know enough from what I saw with my initial run in with those machines. I also knew that I was not strong enough to stop them. So I waited. The riots have been put down now though and we can continue our route into the desert. Hopefully I will never have to return to this place again.
