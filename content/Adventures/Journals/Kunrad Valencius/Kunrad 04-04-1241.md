## Morning

A new day in this city and we needed leads. I had the idea that rebels would be gathering and hoarding equipment and supplies from local markets, me and the [[William Cromwell|Scot]] went to investigate this. The [[Frigg Oden|Pagan]] was on about some pagan superstition, or some other nonsense that her kind is obsessed with. The [[Allistair Ashworth|Singer]] followed her, to ensure she did not repeat what she did, which was likely a proper move. I do not know what the drunkard was doing.

Following my idea we reached upon something: certain stores had very different ledgers. We arrested one to gather information but he was refusing to give information. I proceeded to beat him to an inch of his life, and then healed him to make a full recovery. I threatened to do so again, and he gave up the details: the rebellion had him order more than what he needed and then the rebels would hijack the shipments, "stealing" the extra for their militant uses.

We investigated the warehouse doing the shipping. I do not know if he was involved, the city guards were, or it was just a series of massive incompetency, but this has been going on for months yet nothing had been done to stop it. Yet I did not have time to ponder this fact as I moved to tail a new shipment to catch the ambushers in the act.

We did so and 7 rebels fought me. Of course, as I had the favor of the Almighty himself, it was never even a contest if I would win. The [[William Cromwell|Scot]], the drunkard, the [[Allistair Ashworth|Singer]] and I captured 2 of them alive.

After a short stop to pray at church, we took them to the keep and interrogated them. They were both [[Islam|Saracen]], one clearly a novice and the other more experienced. They at first refused to comply and the latter attempted to spit in my face. I knew then that pain would not convince these fanatics of [[Islam]], their sense of reason and truth had been closed by their perverted zealotry.

I thus had food and drink ordered: roasted ham and wine. I told them to not worry, that we were all friends. We should eat and drink together, as friends do. Of course they did not wish to eat or drink something strictly forbidden to their superstition. I have heard of some sects that preach that consuming pork renders one unfit to enter the realm of their God, though I do not know if this is true.

Seeing them not partake, I went to the experienced one, and had him held down. I encouraged him and when he refused I grabbed it and forced it down his neck, piece by piece. Afterwards, I said he needed to wash it down so I forced the wine down his throat as well. Wine glass after wine glass until he was too drunk to be coherent. I then asked the younger rebel if he wished to tell me about their operation or if he wished to eat as well, and he was quite eager to tell me everything he knew.
