# Session 2 Recap — Shadows of the Black Hand

**In-Game Start Date:** May 29th, 1241 — the Convergence, early afternoon
**In-Game End Date:** May 29th, 1241 — evening, mid-encounter
**Location:** [[4 - Places/Special Locations/Castle Stillhold]] — the battlefield inside the Ward, and the pit beneath it

---

## The Orb Problem, Solved With a Hammer

The session opened on the quiet after the first wave, with the Avatar's unfinished sigils and half-built orb still hanging in the air where it died. **[[William Cromwell]]** looked at the thing nobody understood, hit it with a hammer, and it went straight through — a few sparks, and the whole working dissipated. The soldiers nearby had no idea what he'd just done. Neither does the party.

[[Baldwin Greystone]] arrived shortly after, ignored them, and ordered scouts down into the hole. **[[Kunrad Valencius]]** wanted to go with them; Baldwin told him no — *they need to be quiet, and your armor is not quiet* — and walked off. The party put it to a vote. [[Allistair Ashworth]] voted no, [[Frigg Oden]] voted yes, William abstained on the grounds that he is currently a weapon awaiting direction, and Kunrad cast the deciding yes. Nobody stopped them going in. The scouts made a point of getting well ahead of them.

---

## Stealth, But Loud

The hole dropped past a ledge into a descending tunnel: metal like the ship they found on the mountain, ramshackle stairs and ramps bolted together after the fact rather than manufactured, and a vibration coming up out of the deep earth. Tendrils with yellow-glowing veins thickened along the walls until they coated everything.

Kunrad, on a stealth mission, spent the descent smashing tendrils with his warhammer — metal on metal, a gong on every swing — over Allistair's sustained objections. It didn't matter. Nothing reacted. Allistair kept Fount of Moonlight running for light; Kunrad blessed the whole party.

The tunnel opened into a chamber a hundred yards long, ceiling lost in darkness, walls hung with sacs, and at the far end a glowing orb ten feet across attached to the wall, with about fifteen **Phage thralls** tending it. The three scouts were huddled behind a rock outcropping taking notes. Frigg reached them by magic: the node appears to control everything; the sacs are full of immature thralls, still growing; they were heading back to report and bring Baldwin's forces down. They advised the party to come back up with them. The party voted with their weapons instead, and the scouts finished their notes very quickly and left.

---

## Thirty Seconds

The party attacked on their own terms — [[Wilson Silverlade]] and [[Lucero De La Cruz]] both in the fight — and it was a massacre in their favor. Kunrad opened on a natural 20 initiative and crushed the first thrall in one crit; Allistair's Shatter caught four at once; Wilson debuted dual hand crossbows (new feats, John Wick energy, his words) and dropped thralls with sneak attacks; Lucero flurried two down and redirected an attack into the thrall that swung at him; William, axe still fire-enchanted against creatures that burn, put out roughly a hundred damage a round. Frigg's Spirit Guardians hit six thralls at once and kept grinding every round after. Kunrad's aura quietly ate most of the damage aimed at everyone else, which he did not stop mentioning.

**Sovereign** appeared mid-fight — a projection hovering in front of the orb. *Why do you risk your lives?* Frigg told him she'd seen the future he makes and would rather die than live in it. He had no answer to that and stopped talking.

Then Wilson heard it: something big — many somethings — moving on the unseen ceiling, soft and squelching, like an ape climbing rather than a rat. He shot a kindled arrow upward. The ceiling caught: it was flesh, all of it, and in the firelight the whole cavern showed pods from floor to dark, some already open, with something like fifty more thralls climbing down. Not one creature in the entire fight — not the thralls, not the things above — made a single sound, even dying.

Frigg planted Guardian of Faith beside the orb. The core wrapped itself in tentacles like petals and writhed; its only real answer was a worm of psychic pain through William's skull. On the fourth round Kunrad brought the hammer down with a divine smite and the core burst like a pimple — a necrotic spray that caught William worst, being the one man on the field not dressed for acid rain. Fifteen thralls and the core: four rounds, thirty seconds of in-game time. Sovereign fell out of the air writhing, screaming, flaking apart like burnt paper. The squelching above stopped.

Kunrad said that was easy. Wilson said fifty monsters can't be the whole apocalypse. Lucero, watching Sovereign scream, got the distinct impression the pain was **not sincere**.

---

## "Did You Really Think It Was Going to Be That Easy?"

As they left, laughter rolled through the cavern from everywhere at once. William, Lucero, and Wilson froze mid-stride, each caught in a private vision; Kunrad shrugged the whole thing off and kept walking. What each man saw, only he knows.

Sovereign reappeared behind them in a second form: taller, deeper-voiced, features gone firmly masculine, ears pointed, the whole face sitting on him like a thin layer of clay over someone else. *Did you really think that it was going to be that easy?* The party's collective response was to heckle him. He answered insults with psychic pain — Allistair and Frigg both paid for their contributions. His actual pitch, delivered while stepping through and around them: *Why are you trying so hard to stop me? I want to bring peace to this world. Everyone together —* **"they will be together in my body."**

The party walked out on the monologue. Kunrad threw a sign of the cross over his shoulder. Behind them, the scurrying on the ceiling started up again.

---

## Fifteen Men

They surfaced to a camp that erupted into celebration at the news of the core's destruction — and took a short rest, no more, while Baldwin organized his own expedition. About thirty minutes after his force went down, Frigg felt her leftover spirit guardian below dissipate: it had found something to spend its last strength on.

Baldwin took roughly **seventy-five soldiers, seventy-five mercenaries, and fifteen captains** into the pit. He came back up running, beaten bloody, with **fifteen men**.

Halfway between the hole and the Ward's edge, the thing that had beaten them came up after him: a hulking figure the size of ten thralls, armored, one arm ending in a club, the other in something between a hand and a crab's claw, purple eyes without the Avatar's light — flanked by thralls, with a **fresh Phage Avatar** rising above it.

Baldwin rounded on the party: *You were wrong. I lost a score of men down there. You said you defeated him.* The party pointed out that they said they defeated the ORB; Baldwin did not appreciate the technicality. Then [[Tobias Fenwick]] leaned in and whispered something that visibly broke Baldwin's countenance — Allistair's sharp ears caught it: **[[Lektas]] has fallen. The shipment isn't coming.** Allistair also overheard the name of the man who took the city: **Khalid Al-Nuri** — the same name as the last protected city in the dreamers' vision of the future.

Wilson answered the giant with the strange rifle from the mountain. He charged it, held the trigger, and blew a five-foot hole clean through the thing's shoulder — the whole arm sloughed off, and the kickback put him flat on his back in front of the entire camp. The glowing figures on its side read ten before; they read **seven** now.

Baldwin summoned Kunrad to the command tent. Kunrad refused — twice — on the grounds that he was busy fighting. Baldwin told the party their friend would need their help, sent five mechanical guards to back them up, and withdrew with his officers. The session cut there: the party unrested, the one-armed brute still coming, the new Avatar overhead.
