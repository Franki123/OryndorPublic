**In-Game Start Date:** April 5th, 1241
**In-Game End Date:** April 11th, 1241
**Location:** [[Lektas]], road south, [[Chios]]

---

## New Party Member

**[[Wilson Silverlade]]**, a very short human rogue and former mercenary of the [[Circle of Swords]], introduced himself to [[Kunrad Valencius]] as a fellow crusader seeking a new holy cause. Once an agile infiltrator behind enemy lines, Wilson has since gone soft and overweight, requiring frequent meals to avoid exhaustion. Kunrad welcomed him into the retinue.

---

## The Throne Room — Jeremy Finch

The party returned to [[Castle Lektas]] to report on the battle and collect the construct [[Baldwin Greystone]] had promised them for examination. Captain [[Gregory McTubbs]] informed them that Baldwin had already departed through the South Gate. **[[Jeremy Finch]]**, now sole authority in the castle, directed them to a partially disassembled [[Mechanical Guard]] in the throne room.

[[Wilson Silverlade|Wilson]] investigated the construct and found it was not booby-trapped, but a critical component — the power core — was missing. [[Frigg Oden]] attempted to fit one of her uncharged soul crystals into the empty cavity; the crystal was the right type of component but she couldn't seat it properly. The construct had no reaction presumably since the crystal was empty.

The party debated retrieving a working core from one of the twelve active constructs patrolling the city. Wilson suggested stealing one, but it became clear the core couldn't simply be removed, significant disassembly was required to remove the core from the one in the throne room. Kunrad instead leveraged his [[Order of the Radiant Cross]] credentials and church authority, pressuring the nervous Finch with reminders of the Radiant Cross's fearsome reputation. Finch eventually relented and ordered a guard to lead the party to an active patrol, equip them with a **control bracer**, and send them on their way.

---

## The Control Bracer

The guard captain explained the bracer's four commands: **move/follow**, **attack**, **engage**, and **stop** — emphasizing that the constructs are "dumb as dirt" and will follow orders literally, even walking off a cliff if told to do so. He also revealed the crude targeting method: the command "engage brown people" is commonly used.

Frigg and Wilson both grabbed for the bracer. Frigg snatched it away and took possession. Alistair smacked her hand in protest but ultimately accepted the outcome — as long as Frigg had it and not someone worse.

---

## Departure from Lektas

Both the disassembled construct and the assembled one were placed in church custody. Kunrad used his church connections to requisition 30 rations, while the rest of the party purchased supplies. Frigg bought a camel for Wilson to ride and 50 additional rations for the party.

The group chose to head south to **[[Gravdala]]** and **[[The Blue Mountain]]** — the site from [[Alistair Ashworth]] and [[Frigg Oden]]'s vision of a second "metal barrel" that crashed from the sky. The vision showed the Blue Mountain's light washing away the black corruption from the first barrel. The party reasoned they were not yet strong enough to face the Convergence directly and that the Blue Mountain might hold something that could help.

---

## The Oasis Fire

On the first night out of Lektas, the party spotted smoke in the desert interior. They found five locals at an oasis where a careless camper had created a fire that had spread to the surrounding trees. With no water spells prepared, the party fought the blaze conventionally — passing a bucket back and forth with the locals for several hours. The fire was eventually extinguished and the locals thanked them, but it cost them most of their evening.

---

## Travel to Chios

The party traveled south for several days, passing through [[Aelea]] without stopping. Between [[Athrelion]] and [[Chios]], [[William Cromwell]] noticed tracks diverging from the main road toward the direction of the Convergence. The party noted the tracks but pressed on toward their destination.

---

## The Tavern at Chios

Arriving at [[Chios]] after dark, the party split for the night — Kunrad, William, and Wilson stayed at the local church, while Frigg and Alistair took rooms at a middle-tier inn, with Frigg trading 30 gallons of conjured water for lodging.

At the tavern, Alistair noticed a large, scarred man with a **mechanical arm and leg** — prosthetics built by **[[Darius Ralnor]]**, the very mechanic the party had killed back in [[Morga]]. The man, a former cabin boy of the ship *[[Thunderhead]]*, told Alistair his tale: pirates led by **Captain [[Korvus Bloodreaver]]** had attacked his ship, slaughtered the crew, and cost him his limbs. He'd received the mechanical replacements from Darius who had nursed him back to health and had vowed vengeance on Bloodreaver. This man identified himself as [[Boltar the Barbarian]].

Alistair sensed something was weakening the man's soul, and suspected that the mechanical limbs might be drawing on his life force. Frigg attempted to examine the arm more closely but couldn't determine the inner workings or confirm whether his soul was being drained. The party ultimately decided to move on, unable to help or prove anything.

**48 days remain until the Convergence.**
