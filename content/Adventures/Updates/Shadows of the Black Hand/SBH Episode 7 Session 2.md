**In-Game Start Date:** April 12th, 1241
**In-Game End Date:** April 14th, 1241
**Location:** [[Chios]], road north, [[Athrelion]]

---

## A Detour North

The party woke in [[Chios]] to find [[Boltar the Barbarian]] already gone — the barkeep confirmed he had left early, heading north to investigate a travelling performer rumored to steal souls. With **48 days** remaining until the Convergence, [[Kunrad Valencius]] pressed for a detour. [[William Cromwell]] sided with him on grounds of personal history with pirates. [[Frigg Oden]] agreed to move.

They caught up with Boltar on the road north. He was tracking a semi-famous bard — a solo performer who could play any instrument — suspected of making infernal deals and draining the souls of his audiences. Boltar himself had never seen the man's face; he was working from a secondhand description and a single identifying detail: a tattoo on the bard's back, provided by a source back in [[Chios]].

---

## Arrival in Athrelion

The group arrived in [[Athrelion]] — a modestly prosperous trade town built around a river crossing — in the early afternoon. Boltar announced he had tracked the performer to a specific house, then separated to meet privately with his informants, asking the party to wait and rendezvous at the tavern by nightfall.

The party split:

- **[[Kunrad Valencius]]** patrolled the market to deter petty crime, and briefly glimpsed a figure matching [[Tobias Fenwick]]'s description passing the end of a street. He investigated but found the alleyway empty.
- **[[William Cromwell]]** visited the local church to warn the priest of a soul-stealing performer in town. The priest had heard nothing unusual.
- **[[Frigg Oden]]** went to the tavern, where a show was being set up for that evening. The headliner: **[[Volkmar Rauch]]**, a well-traveled solo performer described by the barkeep as capable of playing any instrument. Tickets were two silver each. Frigg bought three.

Speaking with Greg — the logistics man who had arranged the event — Frigg learned that **[[Tobias Fenwick]]** had brokered the booking. Tobias had connected Greg with Rausch directly, handling scheduling and arrangements. The description Greg gave of Tobias matched the man the party had encountered at the workshop.

When the group reunited, William said Tobias's name through clenched teeth. Kunrad noted he had seen someone matching that description in the market less than an hour earlier.

---

## The Wrong Man

Before nightfall, Boltar reappeared and told the party he had found Rausch — at a specific house in a quieter quarter of town. He led them there and, before Kunrad could knock, put his axe through the front door.

Rausch was gone. The back door was still swinging.

Frigg and William split off to circle the alley. They spotted a figure moving quickly and gave chase — but the man they caught was Philip, a tradesman who had simply been late for a meeting. William grabbed him and demanded answers. Philip protested. William struck him. A guard intervened and dispersed the situation. A while later, as Philip walked away from a meeting, Frigg cast Bane on him.

Meanwhile, Kunrad's war horse — sent the other direction — spotted a man with an unusually large bag entering the main street and tracked him to the tavern. By the time Kunrad arrived, the man no longer had the bag.

---

## Volkmar Rausch

The man was **[[Volkmar Rauch]]** — the headliner himself. Kunrad addressed him in German; Rausch responded with warm, excitable recognition, claiming homesickness and relief at meeting a countryman. Kunrad tested him with a reference to an obscure backwater town. Rausch said he had heard of it. Kunrad concluded he was lying.

Rausch acknowledged knowing [[Tobias Fenwick]]: Tobias had attended one of his shows years ago, liked his playing, and reached out periodically with venue arrangements. He described the relationship as casual and professional. He was cooperative in tone, but something in his performance rang hollow.

Kunrad informed Rausch he was being detained for questioning related to a human trafficking investigation. Rausch protested — not violently — citing his show contracts. Kunrad walked him to the guardhouse using his church writ as authority. The guards were skeptical but deferred to the Radiant Cross.

---

## The Interrogation

In the cell, Boltar confirmed he still could not identify Rausch by sight. The tattoo his source had described was the only identifier — and when Rausch removed his shirt, there was no tattoo. Only a mole.

Frigg asked Rausch directly whether he had ever stolen anyone's soul. Rausch looked genuinely baffled.

Kunrad activated **Divine Sense**. Rausch registered as a **fiend**.

The interrogation escalated. Rausch began to sing — a low, building melody that knocked Frigg, William, and Boltar unconscious. Only Kunrad, anchored by his aura, stayed standing.

While his companions slept, Rausch warned Kunrad quietly: *I do not want to hurt anybody. Let me go and we will be done with this.*

Kunrad responded with his warhammer. Two crits. Two Divine Smites. Rausch was dead before the others woke.

---

## What the Heart Revealed

Boltar examined the body while William guarded the cell. He cut open Rausch's chest, reached into the cavity, and pulled out the heart.

The heart was **etched** — dense markings covering the surface. Boltar explained, without ceremony, that this is what an infernal contract looks like when written into the body. It was why Rausch had registered as a fiend to Divine Sense: he had made a pact with a demon. The contract was literally inside him.

The guards, presented with the heart, had no framework to dispute this. One vomited in the corner.

Back at the tavern, Kunrad confronted the organizer Greg and the barkeep. Greg maintained he had known nothing suspicious — Rausch was semi-famous, the booking had come through Tobias, and he had not questioned a gift. The guards sent word to the local mayor.

**46 days remain until the Convergence.**

*The party prepares for what's next, with Rausch dead, his pact confirmed, his bag unaccounted for, and his patron nowhere to be found.*
