
**In-Game Start Date:** April 26th, 1241
**In-Game End Date:** May 2nd, 1241
**Location:** [[UCSS Lazarus]], [[The Blue Mountain]], [[Gravdalla]], [[Gickeras]]

## Leveling Up

The session opened out-of-character with the party walking through their new seventh-level abilities.

[[Allistair Ashworth]] acquired **Phantasmal Force** — a concentration nightmare spell that forces a Wisdom save or deals 4d10 psychic damage per round for up to ten rounds — and **Counter Charm**, which lets him use a reaction to grant advantage on a save against mind-influencing effects to any creature within thirty feet.

[[Kunrad Valencius]] gained **Aura of the Guardian**, letting him use his reaction to absorb damage from any ally within ten feet onto himself.

[[Frigg Oden]] picked up **Potent Spellcasting**, adding her Wisdom modifier to cleric cantrip damage for a flat +7, and a new Guiding Bolt slot.

[[Wilson Silverlade]] acquired **Evasion** (half or no damage on Dexterity saves) and **Reliable Talent** (treat any proficiency-based check of 9 or lower as a 10).

The GM also introduced two new house rule systems: **Intoxication** (a drink-strength-to-constitution-score counter system tracking buzzed, tipsy, drunk, wasted, and blackout states) and **Obesity** (a persistent condition affecting Wilson's movement, AC, and acrobatics, reducible through sustained in-game effort). Both systems are marked as works in progress. Wilson noted his character had dropped a fatness tier from climbing the mountain.

[[William Cromwell]] was absent and retconned as resting outside.

## What MOTHER Knows

The party was still aboard the [[UCSS Lazarus]] when the session proper began. [[MOTHER]]'s projection filled the bridge screen — the same degraded image from last session, fuzzy at the edges, occasionally slicing through itself with no wound.

When [[Wilson Silverlade]] described the world outside as "beautiful and uncaring," MOTHER's form distorted sharply and turned briefly purple before stabilizing. When [[Frigg Oden]] asked whether MOTHER had fallen from the sky, MOTHER confirmed it. When Frigg asked about the craft breaking apart, MOTHER could not answer — or would not. Her image fractured more dramatically with each question about the fall.

She confirmed: this place is her vessel. Then the sentence stopped mid-clause.

Frigg and Allistair were called on for Constitution saves. Frigg failed and collapsed to all fours, briefly lightheaded. Allistair's knees buckled but held.

---

## A Pre-Recorded Answer

[[Allistair Ashworth]] asked whether MOTHER knew anything that could defeat the darkness.

Her degraded projection disappeared for a beat. When it returned, the fuzziness was gone — sharper, faster, certain. A drawer opened in the console below the screen. Inside: **six cylinders** fitted with a button cap on one end and a blank cap with a small hole on the other, a window in the middle showing purple fluid. Someone had wanted these to be found. MOTHER in this cleaner, more coherent state said:

*"Hello travelers. The thing that will help you the most is right here. These will allow you to defeat the powers that threaten this planet."*

Then the screen cut to static and the original degraded MOTHER returned. She clarified that the coherent version was pre-recorded — made when she was more functional. The recording had no further information to offer.

## Arguments About the Vials

[[Kunrad Valencius]] caught [[Reginald Greystone]] reaching for a vial and wrestled him away from the console. While Kunrad held Reginald, [[Allistair Ashworth]] picked up a vial, confirmed it could be injected or consumed, announced he trusted MOTHER, and injected it into his arm.

Kunrad stated he thought the vials should be destroyed outright. Nobody agreed. [[Wilson Silverlade]] used Sleight of Hand to pocket all remaining vials without anyone noticing until it was established.

The party interrogated MOTHER further:

- On the vials: *"They will protect you."* — *"Uncertain"* on the mechanism.
- *"The darkness is one, not many."*
- The creator of the vials was named **Dr. Goodbarrel** — he died before the vessel fell. He starved to death, despite the ship's cargo bays being full of preserved food.
- MOTHER has been operational for at least **two thousand years**, awake beyond her designed parameters.
- The vessel fell because **the darkness attacked it** in transit.
- She has been awake all this time to watch the **sleepers** — her creators, 2,000+ colonists from somewhere unknown, still aboard in stasis.
- The armory was consumed in the crash.
- All data records have been corrupted from age.
- Her "soul" (core processing location) is classified per standing orders from creators she cannot remember.

[[Frigg Oden]] cast **Mend** on MOTHER's hardware. It produced no visible effect on the projection but MOTHER acknowledged it with something resembling appreciation. **Lay on Hands** could not be applied — MOTHER is a construct without a touchable body.

Kunrad's questioning dragged one final thread loose: *Why did you come here?* — *"Our vessel broke."* — *"What broke it?"* — *"The darkness."*

## Departure

MOTHER's final transmission, in the degraded version: *"Thank you for giving reason to my being awake for so long in the dark and silence. I need to wake the colonists and then I can sleep."*

[[Wilson Silverlade]] noticed a **crossbow-frame weapon** on the way out — the bow assembly was absent, leaving only the stock and trigger mechanism. He took it. Nobody had a frame of reference for what it was.

The screen went blank. The ship began to hum — not structural distress, just the sound of something that had been very quiet finally doing something. The guardian construct stood watching from the corridor as the party exited. Its iris went black as the door closed.

The party took a **long rest** at the summit. The metallic-copper taste that had been present in the air throughout the ascent faded over the course of the night. Frigg and Allistair both received further visions during the rest — the contents were private and went undiscussed.

## The Pards Are Dying

The descent brought the party back through the stone teeth. Before they reached them, they found the same Azure Pards from the ascent — roughly twelve of them — lying in the path. They were alive. None attacked. Their blue glow was gone. Patches of their fur had gone gray, and loose cat hair drifted across the ground in the wind.

Frigg's medicine check established they were **malnourished** — breathing in heavy, labored rhythms, too weak to sustain themselves. The pards that had died fighting the party during the climb had shown unusually low body fat; these survivors were now in collapse. Whatever had been sustaining them was gone.

Kunrad used Lay on Hands on one: no effect on disease or poison. He used direct healing and it recovered enough to stand, then collapsed ten feet away. He carried it down the mountain anyway. Frigg killed one, opened it up, and found nothing the prior skinning hadn't already shown — empty stomach, identical physiology.

Their theory, developed while descending: the blue glow was the ship. MOTHER had been sustaining the Pards — whether as guards, as tests, or as something else — and when she shut down to wake the colonists, the Pards were cut off from whatever energy had been maintaining them.

The mountain itself was no longer blue. Kunrad expended the rest of his Lay on Hands over the course of the descent to keep his pard alive. It arrived at the base breathing but barely.

## Emir Tariq Al-Jabali

The guards at the Gravdalla gate came running when the party appeared. Not armed — surprised. The mountain had been blue for as long as local memory extended. It was not blue anymore. That was considered remarkable by the people for whom it was a religious landmark.

The party was escorted to **Emir Tariq Al-Jabali**, who received them in a formal hall dressed in considerable opulence — rings on every finger, embroidered robes, a crown that fit the occasion rather than exceeded it. He announced they would feast and told them they had earned it.

The party was seated at a thirteen-place head table. An announcer read each name as they entered. Kunrad was announced as **Knight of the Radiant Cross** — the emir's household knew what the armor meant. The emir offered drinks without pressure; Wilson declined with an allergy excuse, Kunrad declined on oath, and the rest drank.

The intoxication counters ran:

Allistair, Frigg, and Lucero drank through the toasts and reached **tipsy** by the end of the night. Allistair was called on first to give a toast — he described the permafrost, the soundless tunnel, and the azure cats. A second drink followed. The emir then called on Wilson for a toast; Wilson gave a deliberate speech about trials overcome and the survival of the group, deliberately omitting any mention of the vials. The emir pressed him on the riches found. Wilson deflected with a speech about divine calling and the true value of life. The emir was satisfied.

By the end of the evening, **Allistair reached Wasted** — the poison condition, half speed, mandatory constitution saves to act. He didn't look well when they went to bed.

The emir housed the party for the night. Kunrad had the sick pard brought to his room. Frigg used **Eyes of the Grave** from her bedroom to detect any undead — nothing in range. The night passed without incident.

The next morning, mining prospectors were already heading up the mountain. Kunrad warned them about the azure pards, the frozen corpses, and the cold. The prospectors pointed out that the djinn had been defeated and therefore the danger was presumably resolved. Kunrad pointed out that the djinn had nothing to do with wild animals. The prospectors seemed wary and delayed their trip.

The party collected Jeff and Stacy and left Gravdalla.

## Reginald Gets a Vial

The road west. Reginald rode with them, composed as always, and waited. At the first overnight stop in **Gickeras**, he made his ask again: he had climbed the mountain with them, fought the pards, kept himself from reaching for the vials when Kunrad physically restrained him. One sixth of the spoils was fair. He would hold it uninjected the same way they were holding theirs — he just wanted it.

The party split on it. Wilson refused outright on grounds that Reginald had no vision, no divine calling, and an estranged father who was directly entangled with the darkness they were trying to defeat. Allistair said to give it to him. Kunrad said it was not Wilson's call to make for everyone. Frigg wanted to wait and see what Allistair's vial did to Allistair first.

Wilson gave it over, reluctantly. Reginald pocketed it without injecting it and thanked him. He said: *"I can appreciate your caution. You are making the best possible choice."*

## What Allistair's Vial Is Doing

By the inn at Gickeras, Allistair was pale and coughing. His nose was running. He had two levels of exhaustion. Kunrad used Lay on Hands to cure disease or poison — no effect. Kunrad could not absorb it through Aura of the Guardian either.

## The Three Hags

Frigg could not sleep. Neither could Allistair. Both spent the early morning hours awake without knowing why.

Frigg rolled a **NAT 20 Arcana check** sitting in her room at 3 AM. The answer arrived as a single clear understanding: she was **tangled in the web of existence itself**. Aligned with reality in a way that normal people are not. In the historical cases she could recall, people with this kind of alignment eventually went mad.

She found Allistair in the hall. He was headed out for a walk. She told him what she'd learned. He said: *"I don't even know what that means."*

She didn't have an answer for him. Then they both heard noise from downstairs.

The tavern's common room at 3 AM: empty, except for three figures crouched around a large iron pot, which was sitting on an open fire on the wooden floor. A man was seated inside the pot. He was alive. He was not fighting it. There were no restraints on him, no obvious reason for his compliance.

Kunrad and Wilson could hear voices from downstairs but saw and heard nothing when they came to look — an empty room and two party members apparently talking to air. Kunrad ran **Divine Sense**: **three undead presences** directly in front of him.

There were three **hags**, existing on a different plane. Frigg and Allistair could see them. The others could not. The hags explained themselves without apology: the man in the pot had broken a contract. They were here on business. They were not in this plane. They had not disturbed anyone on this plane until they were interrupted.

Frigg tried to touch the pot. Her hand passed through. She tried to touch a hag. Same result. She made ice cubes appear inside the pot via **Create Food and Water** — the pot, also ethereal, was unaffected.

Kunrad swung his warhammer at where one of the presences registered. His magic weapon passed cleanly through the air.

This perturbed Allistair who then cast **Suggestion** on Kunrad (go back to your room). Kunrad made the save. Allistair cast **Phantasmal Killer** on Kunrad. Kunrad made that save too. Kunrad, correctly identifying that he had been targeted by two spells, hit Allistair twice with non-lethal strikes: nine damage, then seven more.

Frigg paid the hags **25 gold** to explain what was happening. The gold vanished from her hand. The hag told her:

*"He has ghosts bound to him."*

Three ghostly presences. Bound to [[Allistair Ashworth]]. These are consistent with what Kunrad's Divine Sense had been registering near Allistair since [[Athrelion]].

The silent hag — the one who had not spoken throughout — then addressed Frigg and Allistair directly. She said:

*"You are both bound together. Two sides of the same coin. Fulfilling two halves of the same contract"*

Frigg asked whether that meant if Allistair died, she died too. The hag put out her hand. *"Thirty gold."* Frigg did not have thirty gold. The hag waited. Frigg asked how the contract had been entered without their knowledge. The hag said: *"I don't know. But you entered it."*

Allistair said he didn't remember any contract.

The hags finished their business, muttered about being behind schedule, and went home. The man in the pot was no longer visible. The pot was gone. The fire was gone. The floor was undamaged.

Frigg told Kunrad there had been ghosts, and that he had been hitting them. Kunrad set his warhammer down. Allistair noted that for all their trouble, the hags had made the most sense of anyone in the room. The party dispersed back to their rooms.

## Delta

The party left the Lazarus with six vials — one injected by Allistair, one taken by Lucero, one given to Reginald, and four held by Wilson. MOTHER shut down to perform her final function. The mountain stopped glowing. The Azure Pards are dying. Allistair is symptomatic from the vial in ways Lay on Hands cannot resolve.

Frigg and Allistair have learned they cannot sleep because they are tangled in the fabric of existence itself — a condition that historically ends in madness. They have been told by hags that they are bound to each other by a contract neither recalls making.

**32 days remain until the Convergence.**
