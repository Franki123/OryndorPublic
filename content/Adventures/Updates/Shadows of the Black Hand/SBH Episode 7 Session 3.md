**In-Game Start Date:** April 14th, 1241
**In-Game End Date:** April 25th, 1241
**Location:** [[Athrelion]], road south, [[Gravdala]]

---

## The Curtain Call

The tavern had not yet processed what happened. **[[William Cromwell]]** was standing in the middle of the floor holding a demon's heart, the crowd was beginning to murmur, and the show's headliner was dead in the guardhouse. Before the grumbling could tip into something worse, **[[Allistair Ashworth]]** stepped up onto the stage and started playing.

The crowd went quiet. Whatever he played, it worked — a 28 on the check, enough to make people forget they'd had a bad night. Men who had been reaching for thrown cups found themselves sitting down. **[[Wilson Silverlade]]**, seizing the moment, navigated the crowd with a natural 20 on Athletics and claimed the finest seat in the house: a cordoned VIP table near the front.

While Allistair kept the room, **[[Kunrad Valencius]]** activated Divine Sense. Three undead signatures registered near the stage. A fourth registered at one of the tables across the room. Kunrad moved toward it. But not before looking around.

---

## Tobias Fenwick

He was well-dressed: orange handlebar mustache, monocle, a short top hat placed neatly on the table beside him. He had sat down next to Wilson while the show was playing, watching with the stillness of someone taking notes. Wilson tried to engage him — a measured comment about taste and craft. The man responded with polite condescension and returned his gaze to the stage. Wilson had no way to know who he was.

Kunrad recognized him immediately. So did **[[William Cromwell]]**.

William raged. The two men crossed the tavern floor. William's axe came down on the chair — and the image evaporated. No blood, no impact, no resistance. Tobias Fenwick had not been there at all. Whatever had been sitting next to Wilson dissolved the moment William's blade struck.

Outside, Kunrad's warhorse reported a man exiting through the front door and took three steps before vanishing. No footprints beyond the third. Tobias had somehow been watching through a proxy and was already gone.

Wilson, catching up outside, asked who they had been hunting. He had been sitting next to the answer for several minutes without knowing it.

The party made a swift decision: spending weeks searching for a man who could disappear after three steps was not a use of the days remaining before the Convergence. They would leave in the morning.

---

## What Was in the Bag

While Allistair played his second set, William searched the tavern. He found Rausch's bag behind the curtain at the back of the stage.

Inside: a large vial of red viscous liquid, and a hidden inner pouch containing **52 small vials of a dull bluish substance** — glittering faintly when held to light, streams of something moving within each one. The bag's volume suggested something large was missing. Whatever Rausch had carried as his most significant item was gone.

In the tavern owner's locked room, William found a music box that fit the empty space in the bag: a spinning teacup mechanism, a pleasant melody, supposedly bought for a granddaughter. He took it for examination.

In the morning, the group met with the bishop at the local church.

The blood vial: blood, purpose unknown.

The music box: mundane, nothing magical. Returned to the owner.

The 52 small vials: the bishop held one up to candlelight and went still. He had never seen it in this form. He did not know how it had been put into glass. His identification was unambiguous: *pieces of soul*.

---

## Fifty-Two Vials

Kunrad's position was simple: the souls belonged with God, and God would protect them. He reached into the bag and began crushing the vials in his fist. Small sounds came out of each one as it broke — not quite wails, but close enough.

William objected briefly, then relented. Wilson suggested the vials might be useful as tools. Kunrad asked him directly whether Wilson would like to be held as a tool while someone decided whether to use him later.

**Kunrad smashed all 52 vials and blessed each one as it broke.**

---

## South

The party departed Athrelion the morning of April 15th. [[Boltar the Barbarian]] traveled with them as far as the road fork near [[Chios]], then turned off toward Afador without ceremony. His business in the region was unresolved, but his next source of information had been killed.

At [[Gikaras]] — the last Christian-controlled settlement before the Muslim territories — the group acquired transport: a wagon and an additional camel, christened **Jeff** by William Cromwell to accompany the existing **Stacy**. The road south wound through territory that had never fallen during the Crusade. The architecture was intact, the defenses still standing. Kunrad was unimpressed in the way a man is unimpressed by something he had wanted to destroy and couldn't.

---

## The Flash Flood

South of Gikaras, the road ended at a dry riverbed with no bridge. Wilson found no traps, but signs the bed was overdue for rain. Kunrad identified it as an ambush site — not wrong about the category, just the cause.

William heard the rumbling first. Kunrad cast Bless. Everyone pushed across the remaining distance — William first, Kunrad and the wagon second, Wilson scrambling up the far bank just as the current roared in behind him. The riverbed filled.

---

## The Gates of Gravdala

[[Gravdala]] sits between two mountain peaks — the only gap through a range that extends to both coasts — which is why it never fell to a Crusader siege. The original architecture is intact: walls, minarets, market districts. The party had heard of it as a trade hub. The reality is more impressive than the reputation.

A guard met them at the gate and asked for a jizya — the tax levied on non-Muslim travelers.

The party stood at the entrance to the only city between them and the Blue Mountain.

**34 days remain until the Convergence.**

---

*Gravdala does not fear the Blue Mountain — it simply refuses to discuss it. The scholars know more than they say. The gate guard is asking for money. And somewhere behind them on the road, Tobias Fenwick is adjusting his plans.*

*Session 4 begins at the gates of Gravdala.*
