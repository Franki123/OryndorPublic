# Session 1 Recap — Shadows of the Black Hand

**In-Game Start Date:** May 19th, 1241
**In-Game End Date:** May 29th, 1241 — the Convergence
**Location:** [[4 - Places/Special Locations/Castle Stillhold]]

---

## Bruises From a Place That Hasn't Happened Yet

Evening in the encampment, walking back from [[Baldwin Greystone]]'s tent. **[[Frigg Oden]]** and **[[Allistair Ashworth]]** both went down mid-stride. **[[William Cromwell]]** caught them — a clean twenty, and no real difficulty, because neither of them fell so much as crumpled. Under their clothes, both were covered in bruises minutes old. [[Lucero De La Cruz]], examining Frigg later, recognized them immediately: crush injuries, the kind he'd pulled out from under collapsed buildings.

Frigg woke up and reported that she had been gone about a week. She had been unconscious for several minutes.

What she and Allistair had seen was not a vision — they had been in [[4 - Places/Kingdom of Pseizia/Phitalepia Earldom/Settlements/Lektas|Lektas]], a millennium or two forward. Humanity lost. **The Phage** took the world, and Lektas was the last thing standing: a single protected city ringed by a barrier of black creatures, instant death for anyone who crossed out. The protection was something like **Mother**, tied to a person, and it ran on souls. Everyone inside was half a person — hollowed out, barely able to hold a cantrip. And there were beings from elsewhere in the city, wearing people's faces and carrying forged papers, working to take the protection for themselves. The building came down on Frigg and Allistair; someone detonated charges so the thing could not be taken back.

The one usable fact came out of all of it: **the Phage burns**. Fire is the answer.

William's position was that this was pagan nonsense and that he was nonetheless willing to try setting things on fire. Wilson stress-ate. Lucero called it a great gift and said he believed every word.

## Incense and a Locked Door

Kunrad took it to Baldwin, who was in his tent with **[[Tobias Fenwick]]**. Tobias committed on the spot: flamethrowers, and oil in whatever quantity his sources could move on ten days' notice — "oil enough for a siege is quite a bit." Kunrad added incense and censers to the list, for blessing. Baldwin, wanting him gone, waved a hand and said *why don't you go bless the site*, and Kunrad took that as permission to bless the castle.

The gate guard told him no one was permitted inside. Kunrad talked his way past anyway, and the guard went straight to Baldwin's tent about it.

Inside, past the outer wall with its inward-facing archer slits, censing the courtyard with fifteen mechanical guards ignoring him, Kunrad heard a voice from the great hall. *Hello — is anyone out there?* He ignored it three times, reciting the Lord's Prayer over it, until it resolved into someone screaming for help while other voices worked on them.

He went down into the undercroft.

The man inside was starved, whipped, one toe crushed, and stood in the middle of a circle of sigils with lines running up the walls to five sconces, each holding a cluster of soul crystals. Lay on Hands stopped a foot short of him — a force field, invisible until Kunrad hit it with his warhammer and it flared blue while the crystals brightened. The man said his name was Tom Jerry, corrected it to Tom Frederick, said he was a sailor taken from [[Morga]], said his captors kept talking about a convergence and that when they killed him a gate would open. He asked, over and over, to be let out. *Destroy the crystals and you free me.*

Kunrad did not believe a word of it, mostly on the grounds that no real person has three names, and left to ask Baldwin.

---

## "There Are No Prisoners in the Castle"

Wilson and Lucero tracked Kunrad into the hall by the incense. Outside, William caught Frigg a second time — she'd collapsed again, and came back up with a vision: a black corridor lined with candles, each one whispering a story she couldn't quite hear, and a cloaked figure walking toward her snuffing them out one by one. It met her eyes, then looked down at her hands. She was holding a candle like the others. Then it smiled at her.

The party took the issue to Baldwin, who was planning with Tobias and a lieutenant. He denied it flatly — *there are no prisoners in the castle*, *there is no man in the castle* — and then, pressed on what exactly was standing inside a ring of sigils, gave up the truth: that is what we are fighting. It has many faces. It was here before we were; the castle was built on this site because of his visions, built around the thing **while it was asleep**, and it is waking up now. Anyone who sees a man down there is being tricked.

Kunrad pushed on why they hadn't been told any of this. Baldwin told him to know his place and slapped him. Kunrad slapped him back, and Wilson drew the [[Lazarus Laser Rifle]] and put it on Baldwin, who registered only that it was a crossbow with no strings. Allistair, arriving into the middle of it, talked everyone down: the Convergence is the enemy, and they cannot fight it without Baldwin's army. Tobias put a hand on Baldwin's shoulder. Baldwin conceded that they would be briefed more fully, restated that no one enters the castle — *not for my pride, for your safety* — and dismissed them.

They went straight back into the castle.

Lucero had been left outside the undercroft door and was pulled in. By the time the others reached him he had walked inside, and the man in the circle was no longer a stranger — it was the man Lucero hadn't saved, gutted, on the ground, telling him *you can make this right, just let me go*. He was mid-swing at one of the sconces when William grabbed him and Kunrad hit him in the chest with a warhammer, which did work.

The thing in the circle stood up clean. No bruises, fine plain clothes, features gone soft and androgynous, entirely unbothered. As they hauled Lucero out it said: **"I'll see you soon. All of you."** Kunrad threw *vade retro satana* over his shoulder and it laughed at him down the corridor.

A lieutenant met them outside and asked whether Baldwin hadn't just spoken to them about that.

## Ten Days

Baldwin assigned them a minder: **Lieutenant Sticks**, young, immaculate armor, not at liberty to say how many men he'd killed or what front he'd served on. Kunrad put an arm around him and worked out inside two minutes that he'd never seen a war. Sticks put the party into a mock battle against his own troops with padded weapons — Allistair opened with Shatter, William drew every single attack on the field for two straight rounds and then cleaved three men down, and Kunrad dropped the last archer. Sticks chewed out his troops afterward. Kunrad told him that was the commander's failure, not theirs, and took over their training for the duration.

The rest of it was ten days of work:

- **Kunrad** drilled Baldwin's soldiers daily. They came to respect him specifically — a crusader, a soldier — while continuing to regard the rest of the party as mercenaries, talking behind their backs and going quiet when they walked in.
- **Frigg** worked with the camp mages to cut a trench the whole way around the castle and pack it with wood, brush and anything that would burn, then set posts and hanging braziers of pitch along it on chains. A fire cage.
- **Allistair** wrote a song — *The Devil Went Down to Georgia*, the Caucasus one, thank you — and performed it over the trench diggers for ten days.
- **William** worked the camp for anything better than standard issue and came away with one scroll of enchant weapon from the mages. There is no shop. There is nothing out here but the castle.
- **Wilson** shadowed Kunrad, and found no pitch in the armory — the cooks had oil, under the quartermaster's lock.

From around the fifth day the ground began to shake. By the last day the walls were moving.

---

## Sovereign

On the final day the mages lit the Ward — the staged soul crystals came up and closed a pale blue dome over the castle and the camp.

A man appeared in front of it. Similar enough to the thing in the undercroft to be recognized, different enough not to be the same face. Every eye on the field was pulled to him and a semicircle formed without anyone deciding to walk. Baldwin pushed his way to the front.

> *"My name is Sovereign. Lord of Lords, King of Kings and I have come to reclaim my throne."*

He offered terms: join him and know nothing of this world's pain, or fight him and die. That was the whole of it. Kunrad put three javelins through him during the speech; they passed through an illusion, and it did not so much as look at him.

Then Sovereign started making eye contact.

**William** got his mother, as she was the last time he was home. Everything went black except her, and every memory of his parents came up at once, including the part where they were killed in front of him and he watched his father's eyes go out. He took one step, and then another, and a soldier about the age his father would have been caught his arm, tears streaming down his face and told him *there is no going back, there is only forward, don't do it.* William held his position, but a single tear escaped down his cheek.

**Allistair** got no vision at all. Sovereign smirked at him and moved on, and Allistair felt his mind like a lock being pried open to admit a second key. Disorienting, not disabling. 

About a quarter of the camp walked toward him. They were shot down with crossbows where they stood and left lying there.

Sovereign found Baldwin, held his eyes, and gave him a brief, deeply condescending smile — the one you give a child who won't stop talking. Then the wind took his shape apart like sand, and the castle came down into its own foundations.

---

## The First Wave

The **Phage Avatar** came first: bipedal, four arms in two pairs, black membrane stretched over white musculature, yellow eyes, no mouth. Behind it, **thralls** — shapes of men that were stretched and malformed, moving on all fours, the features of their face sucked into pits, and later **stalkers**, wolf-shaped, snouts deformed, mouths crammed with rows and rows of teeth. Not one of them made a sound, at any point, including when they died.

The Avatar sat down and started drawing sigils in the air with two hands while the other two folded them into a sphere it held in front of itself. It never stopped, all fight, through everything.

The fight, in order of what mattered:

- **Lucero** made a molotov out of a jar of oil and a strip of cloth, doubled his jump, and threw it down onto the Avatar from above. It caught, and burned for the rest of the fight.
- **William** — fire-enchanted axe, raging — set thralls alight as he cut through them, and later switched to the warhammer and put twenty-seven into the Avatar in one swing.
- **Allistair** unloaded **Tale of the Brute** at the perfect moment and knocked every Phage creature on the field prone except the Avatar.
- **Frigg** opened with Guardian of Faith, then dropped **Spirit Guardians** centered on the Avatar and killed more of them than anyone. She was also the only PC to be seriously mauled — a thrall got through and tore into her.
- **Kunrad** held the front at 25 AC and was never once attacked, which he found insulting. He caved in a thrall's chest with the warhammer; black ooze, cracking ribs, no scream.
- **Wilson** stayed hidden and shot from cover, and ate the Avatar's only real attention: a sigil released straight into his mind, purely to hurt him.

Baldwin's mechanical guards held; his soldiers did not. The stalkers went around the line for the isolated ones and tore three of them apart while the party was busy with the Avatar.

The Avatar never finished. It died at the end of its own turn to Frigg's spirit guardians, without ever completing whatever it was building. The battlefield went silent — and the sigils it had drawn stayed where they were, floating, with the half-finished orb still sitting there in the air.

The party celebrated. Kunrad observed that this apocalypse sucked and nothing bad could possibly happen now. Allistair asked whether that had only been phase one.

It had.
