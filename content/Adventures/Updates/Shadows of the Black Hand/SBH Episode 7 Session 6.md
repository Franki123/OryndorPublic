# Session 6 Recap — Shadows of the Black Hand

**In-Game Start Date:** May 2nd, 1241
**In-Game End Date:** May 19th, 1241
**Location:** [[Gikaras]], [[Chios]], [[4 - Places/Kingdom of Pseizia/Phitalepia Earldom/Settlements/Lektas]], [[Castle Stillhold]]

---

## A Letter and a Vote

Morning at the inn in Gikaras, a few hours after the hags left. [[Allistair Ashworth]] was still pale and coughing, two levels of exhaustion deep. A messenger found **[[Kunrad Valencius]]** downstairs with a letter from **[[Sir Alaric Trebid]]** — proper seal, no context — and got a single silver coin for his trouble. Kunrad called it "a tenth of all the wealth I possess." Allistair pointed out it was closer to half a day's wages. Kunrad did not revise the tip.

**[[Reginald Greystone]]** came down shortly after and asked, plainly, what the party knew about his father. Allistair and [[Wilson Silverlade]] told him: they believed Baldwin was tied to the Convergence. Reginald didn't deny it — he reframed it. His father wasn't trying to start the Convergence, he said. He was trying to survive it, at a cost he believed was unavoidable. He named the destination outright: **[[Castle Stillhold]]**, a castle his father requisitioned during the Crusades, sitting on whatever this is all building toward.

With twenty-seven days left on the clock, the party argued over a detour. Reginald had a contact in Lektas — an alchemist who might be able to explain what the vials from the *[[UCSS Lazarus]]* were actually doing to Allistair. Wilson and Allistair wanted to go; Frigg wanted to push straight on. Kunrad, mid-argument, got up and walked out to write letters and didn't come back to vote. Two against one, detour won.

Kunrad's letters turned out to be a small stack — "at least twelve" — which he described, when pressed, as "execution requests" without further detail. This did not reassure anyone.

---

## The Road to Lektas

The party detoured through **[[Chios]]** on the way. Allistair and Frigg still couldn't sleep — no new exhaustion, just the same airless wakefulness that's been following them since the hag encounter. Frigg found a black stray cat in the streets at night and handed it to Kunrad the next morning as a replacement for the one he'd lost. Kunrad's response was a long, disapproving, entirely silent stare, which he himself later described as "verbose."

Eleven days out of Gikaras, the party reached **[[4 - Places/Kingdom of Pseizia/Phitalepia Earldom/Settlements/Lektas|Lektas]]**. The city felt thinner than their last visit — more [[Mechanical Guard|mechanical guards]] on patrol, fewer people on the street.

---

## Anastasia Galeanos

Reginald led Wilson and Allistair to a building by the river that smelled like a struck match. **[[Anastasia Galeanos]]** — buzzed hair, old burn scars, a manner that treated people as interruptions to a more important conversation she was already having with herself — looked Allistair over the moment she heard he'd injected a vial. She never once looked at Wilson.

She examined Allistair without asking permission twice: a glass instrument in the ear, then a needle. He dodged the first attempt. The second one took a chunk of blood the size of a beer bottle's stem out of his arm anyway. Reginald handed over his own unused vial for her to study and didn't ask for it back. She told them to return in three hours.

---

## The Net Closes

While they waited, Allistair and Wilson went looking for Kunrad and found him praying at a cathedral altar — and found **Sir Alaric Trebid** there too, in person. Wilson eavesdropped from cover and heard Alaric offer Kunrad a deal: surrender the rest of the party to the authorities, and "we can sort this whole mess out." Kunrad's answer was that it would be God's will.

Wilson pulled Allistair aside and admitted he'd been quietly tasked by the church to watch Kunrad. They bought disguises — six silver each — and tried to move unnoticed through the city.

**[[William Cromwell]]** turned up around here and folded into the group at the market.

**[[Frigg Oden]]**, separately, heard from contacts that the party was wanted and went to the city guard herself, intending to clear the party's name from the inside. The charges, once spelled out: mass manslaughter by reckless deployment of a war construct — the mechanical man left behind in a Lektas church, which had gone berserk hours after the party departed and killed a family of five — desecration of a consecrated building, and endangerment of the crown's peace.

Disguised, Allistair talked his way past an arrest attempt outside Anastasia's shop on a technicality — the guards had orders to arrest "the first to exit the building," and Reginald, not Allistair, walked out first. Reginald was taken instead (he was later released without charge). With Frigg, Kunrad, and William already in custody, Wilson and Allistair chose to turn themselves in rather than try to break anyone out. The guards, notably, never confiscated a single weapon from any of them. The whole party ended up in the dungeon together. Kunrad tried to invoke sanctuary through the cathedral; it didn't take.

---

## The Trial

An assigned advocate — brusque, time-pressured, clearly wanting this over — laid out the crown's case. Wilson argued there was no basis to convict: the party had explicitly handed the construct off and left before the kill order was given. The advocate's best offer was ten years and a guilty plea. Nobody was interested.

Reginald broke the deadlock by dragging the church father — the priest who'd overseen the construct — into the courtroom, visibly beaten, and getting a confession out of him: sabotage, motivated by revenge for the Crusades. Kunrad's insight told him the man was lying about *something*, though not which part. Reginald also produced a cobbled-together counterfeit control crystal he claimed to have found hidden under the priest's floorboards.

The advocate took the confession and dropped all charges on the spot. Kunrad wanted to keep digging — he believed the priest was innocent and broken into a false confession — but was outvoted by a party watching the clock. The priest's actual fate went unwitnessed.

---

## What Anastasia Found

Allistair went back to Anastasia alone. She'd developed something that eased his symptoms — cough lingering, exhaustion gone. She couldn't isolate what the vial was actually doing to him, only that it was operating below anything she had instruments for. William got the same blood-draw treatment. The party left Lektas with five healing potions from her, and Kunrad's cat, somehow, survived a stint in the dungeon.

With fifteen days left, the party set out for Castle Stillhold — a five-day road.

---

## What Kunrad Knew

On the road, Reginald finally explained his father's thirty-year obsession: a vision telling Baldwin he would need "an army of steel that had already died" to meet the Convergence. Whatever else Baldwin Greystone is, Reginald insisted, he is trying to stop this, not cause it.

Kunrad, in turn, finally cashed in the letters he'd been writing all session: intelligence that Baldwin Greystone has been seizing church resources without oversight and lying to the Order about it. The party also confirmed that **[[Tobias Fenwick]]** — not Baldwin — is the one who actually runs the **[[Black Hand]]**'s trafficking operation.

Faced with all of it, the party voted to set their objections to Baldwin aside until after the Convergence and approach him as allies. Kunrad, the most resistant to trusting Baldwin at all, was outvoted — and pledged his paladin's oath to help regardless.

---

## Castle Stillhold

Ten days before the Convergence, the road opened onto a plateau in the desert with Stillhold's walls above it, and a full encampment spread around the base — soldiers, a smithy, sealed warehouses, mechanical guards at intervals along the wall. Reginald didn't try to sneak in. He walked up, gave his name, and the party was escorted straight to **[[Baldwin Greystone]]**'s tent.

Baldwin — fifties, grey at the temples — was unimpressed at first. He respected Kunrad as a crusader, was openly dismissive of Wilson, and called the rest of the party "entirely insubordinate." Kunrad gave a real speech and renewed his oath in front of him. When William pressed him on a "reckoning" to come for the constructs and the methods once the Convergence was over, Baldwin only smiled and said, "if there is an after."

Baldwin accepted their help on the condition that they follow his orders during the fight specifically — not generally. Kunrad and Allistair agreed to follow any order that was righteous; William eventually accepted this but restated that there would be a reckoning once this was settled. Baldwin gave them quarters and food, asked them not to pick fights with his soldiers, and dismissed them to speak to Reginald alone.

Walking the camp afterward, the party noticed the castle's openings were being resealed and re-cut to face *inward* rather than out — Allistair read it immediately: this isn't a fortress being defended from outside. It's being built to hold something in. The night closed with Allistair singing in the mess tent for a camp full of soldiers who, by every read available, have served together a very long time and trust each other completely. 

They have not been inside the castle yet.

---

*Quarters have been assigned. The soldiers keep their distance and talk when they think no one's listening. Somewhere past the resealed walls, something is being kept in, and ten days remain to find out what.*
