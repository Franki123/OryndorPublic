# Session 3 Recap — Shadows of the Black Hand

**In-Game Start Date:** May 29th, 1241 — the Convergence, evening, mid-encounter
**In-Game End Date:** May 30th, 1241 — mid-morning, Day 2 of the siege
**Location:** [[4 - Places/Special Locations/Castle Stillhold]] — the battlefield inside the Ward, then the camp

---

## Round Two

The session picked up exactly where it left off: the one-armed brute advancing, a fresh **[[Creature - Phage Avatar|Phage Avatar]]** overhead already stitching together a new orb of sigils, thralls and stalkers flanking, three mechanical guards and a freshly-arrived **[[Reginald Greystone]]** in support. The party was short-rested only. Initiative rolled cold — [[William Cromwell]] nearly opened on a natural 1, saved only by advantage.

A round in, the ground itself seemed to hum, and **Sovereign** returned above the field in a new shape: short, elf-eared, a face like clay laid thin over someone else, eyes with pupils this time — unlike the Avatar's. He picked out **[[Wilson Silverlade]]** specifically. *Why do you struggle? You could have an easy life.* Wilson failed both wisdom saves and went under — a trance of feasts, servants, and a castle that was his for the taking. He sat there drooling until [[Allistair Ashworth]] walked over and slapped him awake.

---

## Grinding Through

Allistair's Shatter and Frigg's Spirit Guardians did the bulk of the crowd control — Spirit Guardians alone accounted for several kills across the fight, chewing through anything that stood in the circle. William raged and cleaved through thrall after thrall with Great Weapon Master, complaining the whole time about how much health they'd apparently always had. Reginald took the brunt of the swarm's attention — hit repeatedly, staggered, and openly regretting volunteering, but he held the line and kept swinging. Lieutenant Sticks fought competently and survived the whole engagement.

Wilson broke free of the charm mid-massacre, dropped to one knee in tears, and put a crit through the **Phage Warden** — the one-armed brute — for a huge burst of radiant/searing damage, immediately following it with a second shot that finished it off with a headshot through its exposed core. The Warden went down before it ever landed a killing blow of its own.

---

## The Avatar's Reckoning

That left the fresh Phage Avatar, still mid-channel on its new orb. It fought back hard: a legendary touch attack incapacitated Kunrad for a full round (his armor and Shield of Faith kept him from being touched directly, but the attack still landed), a natural 20 crit sent him flying thirty feet across the field, and a follow-up psychic blast dropped **[[Frigg Oden]]** to 0 HP, breaking her concentration. William went down shortly after from a second psychic hit, stabilized only by Wilson's potion and Kunrad's Lay on Hands.

Kunrad closed the distance again, and as he raised his hammer for the finishing blow, the Avatar shifted its face into the image of his dead mentor and asked him to stop. He failed the wisdom save — charmed, disadvantage on the attack — and swung anyway. The hit landed. The Avatar's core shattered, and the lifeless husk dropped. The field went silent, then the soldiers and captains erupted in cheers. Kunrad immediately used his remaining Lay on Hands to bring both Frigg and William back from the brink.

---

## Aftermath and the Debrief

Baldwin arrived on the field with several mages once the fighting stopped, congratulating the party and promising the men extra rations. The party took a long rest — the first real sleep [[Allistair Ashworth]] and [[Frigg Oden]] had gotten in roughly five weeks. Allistair spent part of the rest on an hour-long spirit-session ritual (with Kunrad and William's help) to secure a new spell.

Kunrad's rest was cut short: Baldwin's lieutenant pulled him into the command tent for a debrief. Kunrad and Wilson reported what they'd noticed — the Phage creatures fight in total silence but coordinate with each other regardless, some kind of psychic/mental attack capability was demonstrated, and their blood runs purple rather than the usual black. Baldwin confirmed **[[Lektas]]** has fallen and the oil shipment is indefinitely delayed, and that his mages are working on the carapace now sealing the hole — a bone-like material carved with runes similar to, but distinct from, the sigils the Avatars have been drawing. Nobody in camp has the Arcana to say exactly how they differ.

---

## Two Dreams

Both dreamers had visions that night — their first sleep in weeks.

**Frigg's:** a grove of healthy silver-bark trees, one bearing a half-healed axe wound. Black tar wells from the cut; a whisper from the roots — not to her, to the tree — offers it water, sun, spring, anything it wants most. The tree refuses, shakes it off. It dies anyway, wrapped in a shroud of black moss. Ten breaths later the shroud splits and something wearing the tree's shape steps out and walks off into the dark. Trees don't walk. It walks anyway.

**Allistair's:** a company of white-clad dancers. A stagehand in black feathers brushes one dancer's arm; the black spreads like ink. A prompt box at the foot of the stage begins feeding her lines — the part she always wanted, her name on the marquee. She kneels at the box and listens. When she rises her costume has gone entirely to black plumage, and she dances better than anyone ever has on that stage, in time with a tune only the feathers hear. She's smiling. Then the prompt box's light swings up, aimed at Allistair's own box, and he's thrown out of the dream.

Frigg's read: *"So we have one who resists and one who embraces, and they both turn out the same."*

---

## Morning After

Day 2 of the siege opened with the infection made visible. **[[Kunrad Valencius]]**, [[Wilson Silverlade]], Frigg, and [[Lucero De La Cruz]] (absent this morning) all woke feverish and sick, each taking a level of exhaustion. William and Allistair remain unaffected from their earlier doses of the cure. Frigg took one of the remaining vials; Kunrad and Wilson both declined for now.

The party split for the morning:

- **Kunrad, William, and Wilson** went to the carapace. Striking it does damage but reverberates back on the attacker — Kunrad and William both traded HP for progress, with visible cracks forming by session's end, but it isn't broken through yet.
- **Allistair** brought a vial to Baldwin's tent and warned him that Sovereign is working through people's deepest desires — Baldwin confirmed his researchers already had a sample, though the camp has no alchemist capable of reproducing it at scale.
- **Frigg** went looting through the camp's storage tents.

The session ended mid-crisis: the kitchen tent caught fire and threatened to spread to the medical tent on the wind. Kunrad tore the kitchen tent down on itself to contain it; William organized moving nearby tents and gear to cut a firebreak. The camp's food stores are a loss either way.
