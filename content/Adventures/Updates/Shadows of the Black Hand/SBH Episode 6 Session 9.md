
**In-Game Start Date:** April 5th, 1241, 7 AM

**In-Game End Date:** April 5th, 1241, 3 PM

**Location:** [[Lektas]], [[Castle Lektas]]

---

## The Battle at Graybridge

The party split to cover two fronts. [[Kunrad Valencius]] and [[Alistair Ashworth]] stayed to hold the bridge against the advancing mob, while [[William Cromwell]], [[Frigg Oden]], and [[Lucero De La Cruz]] left to confront [[Baldwin Greystone]] at [[Castle Lektas]].

Kunrad rode to the front of the bridge on horseback and delivered a thundering speech — warning the militants that the first fifteen to cross his line would not see the end of the day. Alistair followed with a mournful trumpet melody, further demoralizing the rebels before combat began.

When the militants charged, Alistair unleashed **Shatter** across the front line, bloodying most of them in a single blast. Kunrad rode in and killed two militants in rapid succession with his hammer. Alistair cast Shatter a second time — this time targeting the [[Mechanical Man|Mechanical Guards]] and the Guard Captain, exploiting the constructs' vulnerability to thunder damage. He then used **Dissonant Whispers** to break the captain's nerve entirely — the man screamed and threw himself into the river, swept away by the current.

The militant veterans ordered a retreat. But as the rebels pulled back, one of the Mechanical Guards kept pursuing fleeing civilians. Kunrad intervened — and found himself locked in a brutal one-on-one fight with the rogue machine. For nearly a full minute they traded blows, the construct's sword ringing against his shield again and again. Kunrad burned through spell after spell — **Shield of Faith**, **Divine Smite**, **Rebuke the Violent** — taking heavy damage before finally bringing the machine down with a critical hit. The construct was resistant to his hammer and struck hard enough to threaten even his heavy armor.

## The Confrontation at Castle Lektas

William Cromwell stormed through the castle gate, intimidating the tubby guard captain [[Gregory McTubbs]] into letting him pass. Frigg and Lucero followed. They bypassed the war room on the ground floor — where guards were frantically coordinating the city's defense — and headed upstairs.

William kicked open the double doors to the throne room, where **[[Baldwin Greystone]]** sat beside a small, weaselly man in noble garb — **[[Jeremy Finch]]**. Baldwin dismissed Jeremy to his quarters and faced the party with cold composure.

William accused Baldwin of deploying soul-powered abominations. Baldwin denied wrongdoing — claiming he had licenses, certificates, and everything in order — and offered two options: help defend the city, or leave with safe passage. He revealed the constructs were manufactured by **[[Schmidt Mechanik]]** in Germany.

The party attacked. Frigg cast **Spirit Guardians**, engulfing Baldwin in divine energy. Lucero dashed in and struck him — but Baldwin's frame was unnaturally solid, his body refusing to yield like a normal man's. He took the hits without flinching and showed no interest in escalating the fight.

---

## The Visions

As the confrontation reached its end, something broke through. Both **Alistair** and **Frigg** were seized by visions.

**Alistair** saw demons covered in thick reddish-black molasses pressing through the walls toward him. Above Baldwin, a vast figure of terrible beauty spread its wings against the vaulted ceiling — glowing red eyes watching with the patience of something that had been waiting a very long time. Alistair fell to the ground, stunned, and could only mumble incoherently about demons.

**Frigg** saw the **Nav'** — the restless dead of Slavic legend — seeping reddish-black oil from their mouths and fingers as they stumbled through the throne room. Above, she recognized **Chernobog**, the Dark God, lord of death and all that cannot rest, watching from the rafters on black raven's wings. She recovered more quickly, shaking off the vision with effort.

Lucero rushed to Alistair's side. His field medic experience told him the damage was psychological, not physical. Frigg used her divine sight to scan the room for undead — and found nothing. Whatever they had seen was not physically present.

---

## The Departure

After a short discussion the party willingly withdrew. Baldwin poured himself another glass of what turned out to be apricot juice and watched them leave with weary frustration. Frigg swiped his bottle on the way out. William declared he hoped Baldwin could live with his conscience "when it comes out that these are the souls of innocent."

Back at the tavern, the party regrouped. Kunrad arrived battered and bloodied from his fight with the machine. 

Lucero talked with injured off-duty guards at the tavern and learned that Baldwin had personally shipped in the Mechanical Soldiers and trained the garrison to use them. The rank-and-file knew almost nothing about how the constructs actually worked.

William sent a letter via messenger to **[[Eadric McDougal]]**, asking about Chernobog and informing him of the Convergence and its location.

The party decided to leave [[Lektas]] rather than remain under Baldwin's authority. As the riot slowly collapsed around them, they gathered their things and headed for the city gates.

---

## What Was Learned

- The **Pope** refused to sanction the Mechanical Soldiers and ordered them disassembled — Baldwin is deploying them without papal approval
- The constructs are manufactured by **[[Schmidt Mechanik]]** in Germany
- Both Alistair and Frigg experienced disturbing visions of demonic figures that they connected to Baldwin — but no undead were detected nearby
- The Convergence draws closer
