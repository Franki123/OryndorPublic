# Session 1 Recap — Sovereign's Grasp

**In-Game Start Date:** May 30th, 1241 — the camp outside [[4 - Places/Special Locations/Castle Stillhold|Castle Stillhold]], immediately after the battle
**In-Game End Date:** ~June 11th, 1241 — arriving by ship at [[Stymphid]]
**Location:** [[4 - Places/Special Locations/Castle Stillhold|Castle Stillhold]], [[Sinolis]], [[Cortaris]], [[Stymphid]]

---

## Thrown Out

**[[Exarch Rufus]]** now holds the castle around the hole, and he wanted the party out of it. His faceless legionaries escorted them to the gates. Up close they were humanoid, assembled from borrowed parts: scaled hands, pointed ears, blank faces. Their guns were only gun-shaped, grown onto the bodies carrying them. [[Frigg Oden]] asked Rufus for a handshake and was refused, so she Vampiric Touched the two legionaries dragging her out and took back 17 HP. They didn't react. [[Allistair Ashworth]] asked one what it thought of "the mother." It didn't react either. The gates closed behind them.

Outside, the camp's mages tried to raise the shield again. Partway through the casting, an inch-wide yellow glyph opened under the lead mage's feet and pulled him into it over about five minutes, crushing bone the whole way. When his head went through, the screaming stopped and a stake with a skull on top stood in the camp. Allistair's Starry Wisp fizzled against the castle's field. The mages dropped the shield idea. By the time the party left there were three skull spikes outside the walls.

---

## The Tent

[[William Cromwell]] and [[Wilson Silverlade]] went to **[[Baldwin Greystone]]**'s tent. Baldwin was conscious and pale, his arms webbed black and the veins at the base of his neck. **[[Reginald Greystone]]** held his father's hand. **[[Tobias Fenwick]]** stood off to the side, though he held no rank in the camp.

Wilson offered fire. Baldwin refused and told Tobias it was time. Tobias asked the party to leave "for this part." William refused to go until Tobias answered for [[James Cromwell]]. Tobias honestly couldn't place the name at first: something about shipping, fish. When William accused him of helping kill his parents and sell him into slavery, Tobias asked whether killing him would bring them back. William punched him. Tobias didn't dodge, pushed his own nose back into place, and said he didn't remember being involved. William drew his axe and opened Tobias's shoulder. Tobias didn't dodge that either.

Reginald broke the stalemate. He didn't defend Tobias. He told him it was time they all answered for their sins. Tobias, more annoyed than anything, said he had arranged passage *"to [[Morga]] through [[4 - Places/Kingdom of Pseizia/Phitalepia Earldom/Settlements/Lektas|Lektas]]. But I suppose you don't want me to help any more."* Then he was gone. Where he'd been standing, Wilson found only a small fall of very fine ash. He bottled it.

Reginald explained afterward: Tobias was his father's logistics officer for decades, and very few deals don't touch him. William's family was the defining event of James Cromwell's life and an ordinary Tuesday in Tobias's. *The tree remembers; the axe forgets.*

William then put Sovereign's memory of Baldwin dealing with James to the man himself. Baldwin didn't deny it. *"I'm the one responsible. If you need to kill me, then kill me and be done with it. I know what I've done. I'm not a good man. I did what I felt was necessary."* William told him it might have been forgivable if he'd succeeded, that living with the failure was a worse punishment than anything he could deal out right now, and walked away. His purpose now is hunting down Tobias.

---

## The Camp Comes Apart

Command fell to Reginald, who stayed in the tent with his father. Soldiers grabbed what they could carry and walked. Allistair climbed to a high point, cast Thunderwave for attention, and started a speech: *"We must not lose hope. We cannot let Sovereign beat us."* A yellow glyph lit on his chest at the word "Sovereign" and he dropped mid-sentence. He woke later with a splitting headache, unable to cast spells until he rested. It did not help morale.

Then the medical tent emptied. Burned survivors came out alive and preaching: they had been *yanked from the womb of Sovereign's might* and everyone needed to join. William decapitated one, and Wilson put a bolt in the other as he ran. Inside, a handful of patients strained against their restraints, several more lay comatose, and at the far end lay **Lieutenant Sticks**, awake and quietly crying. *"I was asleep and I can't remember my father. I know what he did for me. But I can't remember his face."* While he slept he could feel Sovereign, and he thinks it was eating him.

Frigg, reasoning that the comatose would be left to starve once the camp emptied, killed them. That left two beds unaccounted for. Frigg and William each tackled an ordinary deserter, and Wilson dropped four fleeing figures with his hand crossbows. Only one of the four had burn marks. One got away.

---

## Jeff and Stacy

Allistair went to check on the camels and found them gone. Jeff and Stacy had been stolen, along with most of the party's rations and the healing potions. Kunrad's kitten went with Jeff. Allistair (nat 20) and Wilson picked the right trail out of eight: Jeff has a limp nobody had noticed before today. Wilson collected an especially rusty dagger for the thief, and the party left the camp behind.

The trek to **[[Sinolis]]** took three or four days through desert that was hotter by day and colder by night than it should be. Frigg's Create Food and Water covered the shortfall. Deserters had already passed through and told people what they'd seen, so doors shut as the party arrived. A villager confirmed a limping camel had come through a few hours earlier.

They ran him down by sunset: one man, two camels. Frigg's Sacred Flame set him alight, and Wilson's nat 20 put two bolts in his back. He was dead. William cut off a foot before Frigg paid 500 gold and her only 5th-level slot to cast Raise Dead, and the others took the rest of his limbs during the hour-long casting. He woke as a torso and a head, and started screaming.

He was not the thief. He'd bought two of three camels from a big-eared man and left town. One was Jeff. The other was not Stacy, though it was apparently a camel. Allistair named it **Derek**. William's Animal Handling says Derek is female. Allistair's Persuasion beat it, and the party inventory now lists "a girl camel named Derek." The buyer, now **Nugget**, guessed the other camel had gone to [[Cortaris]].

---

## Sinolis, Again

The party went back through Sinolis with Nugget bagged on Jeff's back, his head showing through the top. Frigg warned him that any noise would cost him more body parts, and it didn't take. The moment Jeff's hooves hit the stone of the square he started shouting: *"Help! Help! They're killing me!"* William clamped a hand over his mouth. After a brief debate about cutting out his tongue, they stuffed his mouth with a panther pelt, pulled the hood over his head, and went back to knocking on doors.

The first door had seen no one *"since you passed through"* and offered a vague "maybe west." Allistair said this person hadn't done anything to them, and the party spread out to knock on more doors.

Allistair's next knock was answered by a very old woman, about 80. *"Yes, what do you want?"* He asked about a man who'd come in with three camels and left with one. She looked him over, then looked at Jeff for a long moment. *"No."* She slammed the door. He knocked again. *"What do you want? Go away!"* *"Why do you need three camels? Isn't two good enough?"* He explained the thief was an army deserter. *"Don't care about no army."* The door slammed a second time.

Allistair tried to kick it in and bounced off. William put his whole weight into one kick beside the lock, and the door burst open with a bang. Inside was a comfortable house, with pictures on the walls, and its owner, holding a frying pan. William tried to intimidate her: *"Just tell us where the camel guy went."* She was not intimidated and hit him over the head with the pan. *"No. Now get out of my house!"*

William wrenched the pan out of her grip, and she kicked him in the shin. He said they'd leave once she told them where the camel guy went. *"No, I'm not giving you nothing now."*

That was when Wilson's rage arrived. He tore through the house: cabinets, furniture, the bed flipped over. Allistair told her she should have just answered, and William told her she was bringing this on herself. Wilson announced he was cutting off her hand. Instead, with his back to her, he threw a rusty knife across his body and behind his hip without looking. It took her in the stomach. She went down swearing at all of them.

William said they probably shouldn't murder an old lady. Wilson tossed a healing potion onto the overturned bed on his way out. She told him to drink it himself and threw it at his back, and he spun and caught it on a natural 20. Frigg stabilized her. Wilson's position was that the camel had been stolen, so the party were the righteous ones here. Nobody in this town had stolen anything from them. They left her on her floor, shouting after them.

The next door over answered before anyone finished the question. *"They went to Cortaris. They definitely went to Cortaris. Please leave, please don't kill me."* Allistair asked if that was really so hard. *"No, it wasn't. It was great. I actually enjoyed it."* Promised ten stars if they would just go away, the party went.

On the road to Cortaris, William took Wilson aside: *"I know you're upset about the camels, but we don't just hurt the innocent. Save that for the people who deserve our wrath."* Wilson gave him a silent nod. Frigg wasn't happy about it either. She'd have healed the woman properly if she'd had the spell slots, and she figured an old woman like that has a family who'll need her. Allistair held that she'd been obstructing a rightful investigation and had more or less done it to herself. William allowed that she had technically attacked first, though only after they broke in. Wilson called it a court of law at the end of the world. To Sinolis, a band of strangers had kicked in a door, maimed an old woman, and dragged a man through the square screaming for help. They had looked a lot like bandits.

---

## Cortaris

The gate guard at **[[Cortaris]]** asked about the head-shaped lump in the bag. Frigg called it Allistair's top-secret new instrument, and the guard wanted to search the caravan. William produced his clan signet and the guard let them through, adding that he'd inform the local lord.

At the market, Nugget shook his head at all four camel dealers. William found a camel he was 85% sure was Stacy. The dealer claimed she came from a breeder until William's intimidation shook loose the truth: he'd bought her from a brown, curly-haired man who then boarded a ship. William paid him 45 gold for her. The healing potions weren't with her.

They took rooms. Nugget went up the stairs unconscious in William's backpack, and Frigg hand-fed him a very good pie, which unsettled him more than anything else had. **[[Kunrad Valencius]]** stayed at the local church.

The next day Allistair offered Nugget anything he wanted, and Nugget asked for two gold's worth of food. Wilson paid a thieves' guild contact to find the thief. William wrote to **[[Edric]]** and **[[Thora]]** that he'd found his parents' killer. That night William snapped Nugget's neck in his sleep. He regretted what they'd done to the man and didn't expect to be forgiven for it. 

He buried the body unmarked in the town graveyard. A little girl in a nightgown found him digging and asked if he was a bad man. He told her he tries to be good but fails frequently. *My daddy says bad men go to hell,* she said, and went home. He didn't sleep again before sunrise.

Nobody ever asked Nugget his name.

---

## A Name and a Ship

By morning the thieves' guild had an answer: the thief is **Carlyle**, and he took a ship to [[Stymphid]], two days' sail and about a day ahead. Wilson pitched hiring an assassin. Frigg and Allistair would rather do it with their own hands. William would rather be hunting Tobias. Talk of the bigger picture went nowhere: the Convergence has already happened, and nobody has a plan for stopping the end of the world. Reginald had last been headed for Stymphid himself.

They booked passage and arrived in Stymphid.
